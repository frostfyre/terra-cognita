# UE5/Metahuman virtual volumetric capture work

The more recent [[Impossible]] contracting work — UE5.x and Metahuman-based virtual volumetric capture pipelines. Ended fairly abruptly just prior to gaining [[Sentient Machines]] as a client.

## What this was

Building pipelines at the seam between **physical human capture** and **synthetic Metahuman parametric representation**:

- **Metahuman** — Epic Games' high-fidelity digital human creation framework; parametric, rigged, designed for real-time rendering in Unreal Engine
- **Volumetric capture** — physical multi-camera capture of real humans producing photorealistic 3D reconstruction
- **The combination** — using volumetric capture data to drive or inform Metahuman representations; bridging measured reality and synthetic parametric models

This is **the physical/synthetic seam in its most direct form** for human subjects — the same seam Paul navigates in the Sentient Machines sim2real work, but here expressed through Epic's tooling and applied to human performance capture rather than robotic training data.

## Why the abrupt ending matters

The work ended shortly before the Sentient Machines engagement began. Two possible readings:

1. **Causal**: the Sentient Machines opportunity displaced the Impossible work — Paul chose the new engagement over continuing the Metahuman work
2. **Sequential**: the Impossible work wound down, opening space for Sentient Machines

The UE5/Metahuman pipeline expertise is almost certainly part of what made Paul legible to Harry as a hire — the synthetic-data-for-ML-training framing maps directly onto the kind of human-representation pipeline work Paul had just been doing at Impossible.

## The physical/synthetic axis

This work, combined with the [[Radiant Images 4D capture collaboration]], gives Paul experience on **both sides of the physical/synthetic data divide**:

- **Physical** (Radiant) — starting from reality, maximizing capture fidelity given budget
- **Synthetic** (Sentient Machines) — constructing reality procedurally, maximizing variation and annotation
- **Bridge** (this work) — using physical capture to inform synthetic representation; the seam itself as the work

That's a complete axis of experience. The sim2real framing at Sentient Machines benefits from understanding all three positions, and Paul has worked at all three.
