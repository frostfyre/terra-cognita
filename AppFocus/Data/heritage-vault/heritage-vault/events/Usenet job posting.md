# Usenet job posting incident

How Paul found his way to [[VR1 Jaleco]] around Y2K — through a job posting on Usenet, which was the pre-internet-forum professional networking mechanism of the era. Paul's characterization of the situation at the studio: *"pretty sad, as I recall."* Another instance of the frontier-seeking autodidact finding opportunities through non-mainstream channels before the mainstream had caught up.
