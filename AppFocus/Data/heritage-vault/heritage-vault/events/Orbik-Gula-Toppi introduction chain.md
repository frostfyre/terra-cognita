# Orbik → Gula → Toppi introduction chain

A three-hop introduction sequence and the first explicitly-mapped multi-hop chain on the heritage map.

**[[Glen Orbik]]** introduced Paul to **[[Tim Gula]]**, who was a life-drawing instructor Paul then attended regularly at the [[Blizzard library and academy]]. **Tim Gula** subsequently introduced Paul to the work of **[[Sergio Toppi]]**.

## Why the chain matters

The chain is itself a heritage artifact. Without Orbik's introduction:

- The Gula relationship might not have formed in this form
- The Gula-as-life-drawing-anchor at Blizzard might have been different or absent
- The Toppi discovery — a significant passive influence — might have come later or through a different vector, or might not have come at all

**Access to certain figures came through a specific chain of introductions.** The chain is part of the inheritance, not just the destination.

## The function this opens up

**`introduction-chain-link`** — a figure who is less important as a direct aesthetic influence than as the vector through which other important connections were made. Orbik is a real and significant illustrator in his own right; his primary function on this map is as the person whose introduction was structurally load-bearing.

This function probably applies elsewhere on the map once we look for it. Every introduction — Scharmen getting Paul into Blizzard, Cranford running the academy that brought in Gurney and Huston and Gula, Gula introducing Toppi — is a chain-link function, and the chains are load-bearing.

## Implication for the map's architecture

Multi-hop chains like this one are what make the graph interesting as a graph rather than as a list. In Gephi, this chain will be visible as: Orbik → Gula → Toppi, with Gula as the highest-betweenness node in the local cluster (connecting Orbik's noir-pulp tradition, the Blizzard academy, the life-drawing practice, and Toppi's Italian-comics tradition in a single node). Orbik is the entry point; Toppi is the terminus; Gula is the bridge.
