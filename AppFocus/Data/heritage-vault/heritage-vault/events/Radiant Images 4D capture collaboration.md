# Radiant Images 4D capture collaboration

Paul and [[Maxime C Peroumal]] worked with [[Michael Mansouri]] at [[Radiant Images]] on a 4D capture volume project, **concurrent with the [[Hypixel Studios]] chapter**. Paul's framing: *"physical data capture pipelines to the maximum possible given budgets."*

**The collaboration seeded the concept for Radiant Images' portable volumetric capture studio** — which shipped, won awards in 2025, and remains the only product of its kind.

## What 4D capture is

Volumetric video: a subject captured from multiple synchronized cameras simultaneously, producing a three-dimensional representation viewable from any angle and playable through time. The fourth dimension is time. Used in XR, virtual production, high-end visual effects.

## The product genesis chain

1. Paul and Maxime collaborate with Michael on the 4D capture volume concept
2. The work inspires the direction for a portable volumetric studio
3. Michael hoped Maxime might help underwrite the technology
4. Maxime didn't — his spending patterns made it unavailable
5. **Apple introduced genlock to the iPhone Pro line** — enabling precise multi-camera frame synchronization on consumer hardware
6. Michael recognized the moment was too hot to pass up and built independently
7. The portable volumetric capture studio shipped and won awards in 2025

**The genlock pivot** — without precise frame synchronization, a multi-iPhone capture rig produces degraded 3D reconstruction. Apple adding genlock to the iPhone Pro line made a portable, consumer-hardware-based volumetric studio viable at a revolutionary price point. Michael had the technical foundation, market relationships, and selective opportunism to move first. No other product like it exists.

## Paul's contribution

Conceptual substrate and early collaborative development — not the final builder, but the early collaboration that established the direction. **Direct contribution to product lineage** even if not direct builder of the final product.

## The physical/synthetic symmetry

This physical capture pipeline work complements Paul's synthetic data pipeline work at [[Sentient Machines]] — both are about producing high-quality 3D data for downstream consumption. Physical capture starts from reality; synthetic data constructs reality procedurally. **Paul has deep experience on both sides of the physical/synthetic divide**, which is precisely the relevant expertise for sim2real work where that divide is the operative problem.
