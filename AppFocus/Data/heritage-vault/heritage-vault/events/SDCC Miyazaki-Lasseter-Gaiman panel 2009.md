# SDCC Miyazaki-Lasseter-Gaiman panel 2009

**Hall H, San Diego Comic-Con, 2009.** The *Ponyo* promotion panel featuring [[Hayao Miyazaki]], [[John Lasseter]], and [[Neil Gaiman]]. Paul was in the audience — one of hundreds. Miyazaki's only western public appearance.

## Why this is a heritage event rather than just an attendance note

Three figures who all appear independently on Paul's heritage map were on stage together in a room Paul was in. That's a real convergence:

- **Miyazaki** — the threshold-spaces-and-frisson visual aesthetic, concept and storyboard art, the Moebius downstream convergence
- **Lasseter** — Pixar career story as role-authoring-at-institutional-scale, the organizational answer to the Chuck Jones diagnosis
- **Gaiman** — literary legitimization of comics as adult form, Sandman's comparative-mythological scope, work ethic as public-exemplar

Each of these figures appears on the map through separate heritage threads. **The panel is the one moment they occupied the same room Paul was in.** Not a personal encounter with any of them — Paul's anti-inflation register correctly notes *"just one of the hundreds"* — but real witnessed presence with all three simultaneously.

## The Chuck Jones parallel

The VTEL sessions at Sony placed Paul in the room with Chuck Jones diagnosing the mechanism by which institutional capture degrades creative work. The SDCC 2009 panel placed Paul in the room with three figures who each represent different responses to that diagnosis:

- Miyazaki — built Studio Ghibli as a director-controlled studio where artistic vision was structurally protected
- Lasseter — co-built Pixar with the same commitment (before the complications)
- Gaiman — maintained independent creative voice while navigating the comics and publishing industries

The Jones VTEL moment named the problem. The SDCC 2009 panel was a demonstration that different solutions to it existed at scale.
