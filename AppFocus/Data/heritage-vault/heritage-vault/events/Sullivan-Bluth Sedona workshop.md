# Sullivan-Bluth Sedona workshop

**Sedona, at the [[Sedona Arts Center]]**, during Paul's [[Sony San Diego]] years (mid-1990s). A touring or outreach workshop by Sullivan-Bluth animators. The animator(s) running it are not recalled by name.

**Paul was visiting or passing through Sedona at the time** — not yet living there. Full-time Sedona residence came decades later. The workshop happened in a place Paul would eventually choose as home, before he knew it would be home.

**[[John Scharmen]] was also in attendance** — another shared formation experience. Paul and Scharmen attended [[Glenn Vilppu|Vilppu]] sessions together at Sony; they attended this workshop together in Sedona during the same general period. The [[shared-formation-produces-recognition]] pattern: same people, same animation-craft formation context, same early-career window.

## The pre-home Sedona detail

The workshop is one of Paul's earliest known connections to Sedona — visiting the place during the Sony years, absorbing Sullivan-Bluth's classical-animation tradition there, and eventually settling there decades later. The somatic recognition of Sedona as home-landscape (the [[frisson-between-extremes]] red-rock / desert / riparian register) may have had early roots here that weren't consciously identified as such at the time.
