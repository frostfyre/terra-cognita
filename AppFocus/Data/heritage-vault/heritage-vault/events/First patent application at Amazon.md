# First patent application at Amazon

Paul filed his first patent application during the [[Amazon Game Studios|Amazon]] years. **Filed but not granted; remains his only patent application to date.**

## Why it belongs on the map

Not as a credential — the application wasn't granted, and the map should match Paul's voice on this rather than inflate it. **The function is in the *experience*, not in the legal artifact.**

Patent filing at a tech company of Amazon's scale is real work: working with patent attorneys, articulating an invention precisely enough to satisfy USPTO claims structure, navigating prior-art searches, defending the novelty of the contribution under examination. **That's its own kind of formative experience.**

The function-tag this opens up: ***the discipline of articulating a technical contribution precisely enough to file.*** Same disposition as authoring a job description, writing the [[Building the Matrix]] document, articulating the synthetic-data positioning argument. **Patent filing as [[role-authoring]]'s technical-output sibling** — the same authorial relationship to one's own contribution, applied at the artifact level rather than the role level.

Whether the USPTO eventually validated the claims is downstream of the formative experience. The formative experience was complete at the filing.
