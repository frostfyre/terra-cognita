# Spectrum Fantastic road trip

Road trip to **[[Spectrum Fantastic Art]]** in Kansas City with [[John Polidora]] and [[John Scharmen]] during Paul's Blizzard years.

**The detail that crystallizes Paul's actual position in the fantasy-painting field.** Spectrum Fantastic was, for the years it ran, *the* gathering of the contemporary fantasy and SF illustration field. Not a comics con or a gaming con — the *painters' convention*. Driving to it with two colleagues is a pilgrimage, and pilgrimages tell you what someone takes seriously.

Paul wasn't passively absorbing fantasy painting heritage; he was actively traveling to be in the room with it.

Encounters from this trip captured in the map:

- **Breakfast with [[Donato Giancola]]** — substantive conversation, not a signing line
- **[[JB Monge]] and [[Paul Bonner]] sharing a booth** — perfect pairing, structural rhyme to Paul's De Lint side and his Citadel-via-Gibbons side
- **[[Chris Sanders]]** — also at the show that year (recurring familiar from SDCC and LBX)
