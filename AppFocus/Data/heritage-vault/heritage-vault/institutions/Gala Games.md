# Gala Games

Web3 gaming platform, founded 2019. Part of Paul's post-Amazon turbulent stretch alongside [[FTX Good Luck Games]], [[Flickplay]], and [[Hypixel Studios]].

Gala has had a long and turbulent internal history — founder disputes, regulatory questions, and the broader contraction of the Web3 gaming thesis. Another speculative-frontier chapter in the post-Amazon arc.

## Named figures from this chapter

**[[Michael McCarthy]]** — lead designer who cited [[Patrick Rothfuss]]'s books as the inspiration for the world mechanics. This citation sent Paul to Rothfuss, eventually feeding into [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen|Jackalope Junction]]'s Lucius character via the [[Lucius confluence]].

**[[Matthew Bell]]** — Art Director; self-published fiction writer; recommended Paul check out Hytale. May be the named human cause of the [[Hypixel Studios]] chapter — pending confirmation of whether Bell's recommendation was what led Paul there.

This makes Gala the only post-Amazon turbulent chapter with both a documented **creative output** connection (McCarthy → Rothfuss → Lucius) and a possible **forward institutional connection** (Bell → Hytale → Hypixel). A game development job produced a character in a fairy-art venture via a literary recommendation, and possibly also opened the door to the next studio chapter.

## Connection to the broader post-Amazon pattern

The post-Amazon turbulent stretch ([[FTX Good Luck Games]], [[Flickplay]], Gala, [[Hypixel Studios]]) was characterized by speculative-frontier bets in Web3 and crypto gaming, most of which didn't materialize as stable working environments. What the map has recovered from this period:

- FTX/Good Luck Games: followed [[Jeremy Cranford]] (trust-network move)
- Gala: [[Michael McCarthy]] → Rothfuss → Lucius creative thread; [[Matthew Bell]] → possible Hypixel connection
- Hypixel: still partly unmapped beyond the Bell connection

The [[high-trust-relationship-architecture]] concept predicted there should be named human figures underneath each post-Amazon chapter; McCarthy and Bell are those figures for this chapter.
