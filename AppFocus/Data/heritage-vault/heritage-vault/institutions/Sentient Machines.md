# Sentient Machines

Stealth-mode robotics startup founded and run by [[Harry OConnor]]. Paul's current exclusive engagement. The chapter wraps end of May (2026), with the next-phase positioning conversation actively in process.

## What Paul does here

Synthetic data pipelines for ML and robotics training. The full architecture is documented in [[pipeline_context.md]] — SeedTree hierarchical seed derivation, SpatialContext interface, batch ID hashing, manifest design, versioning strategy, domain randomization, object generators, scene composition, render backend via USD/UsdGeom and Newton/IsaacSim.

**This is the convergence point of Paul's three training streams:**

- **[[autodidact-foundational-stream]]** — procedural generation, rendering algorithms, understanding-the-mechanism-underneath, studied since the mid-90s
- **[[Blizzard Entertainment|Blizzard visual-craft formation]]** — visual fidelity, art-direction-driven pipelines, MMO-scale content production
- **[[Amazon Game Studios|Amazon software-and-ML formation]]** — software-engineering disciplines, production-ML, enterprise-AI architecture

None of those three streams alone would be sufficient for this work. All three together are precisely what it requires. The [[dual-training-convergence]] concept was written to capture this; Sentient Machines is where the convergence is actually being applied.
