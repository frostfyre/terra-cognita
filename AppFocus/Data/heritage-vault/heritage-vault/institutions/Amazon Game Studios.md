# Amazon Game Studios

The chapter immediately after [[Blizzard Entertainment]] (Legion ending ~2016). Roughly six years; the longest single chapter after Blizzard.

## The named human cause (the pull)

**Paul was hired in by [[Joey Ray Hall]]**, who had been a huge presence at Blizzard before moving to Amazon. This places the Amazon move firmly inside the [[high-trust-relationship-architecture]] pattern — *Paul moves between studios primarily through trust relationships, not market-driven decisions*.

Paul's stated orientation toward Joey Ray Hall: ***"I wanted to learn from him."*** The Amazon chapter is therefore at least partly **a deliberately-chosen apprenticeship**, not just a job.

## The push side

Earlier draft had only the pull. The Blizzard chapter actually ended *involuntarily* — Paul was canned by an absentee boss whose authority depended on dangling unattainable career-progression carrots, when Paul stopped caring about them. See the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] node for the full mechanism.

Both sides matter: the Amazon move was **a respected colleague to follow toward, *and* an involuntary chapter ending to move forward from**. Reading either side alone misses the texture. The [[high-trust-relationship-architecture]] still holds; the trust pattern is real and Joey Ray Hall is real. But the chapter transitions on Paul's career arc include forced endings as well as pulled beginnings, and the map should hold both.

## Structure of the chapter

- **Marketing as a tech artist** at first. Unusual placement — not standard career path out of Blizzard tech art. Either Paul authored the role on arrival or someone (likely Joey Ray Hall or someone he placed Paul with) understood that marketing for game IP needs production-quality real-time content, captures, demo builds, and that the person doing that work needs to be a tech artist. Another instance of [[role-authoring]].
- **Back into game production** for what shipped as **[[New World]]** — AGS's longest, most public, most painful development cycle. Multiple delays, fundamental design pivots (PvP-focused MMO → PvE-leaning MMO), reception swings post-launch, very public scaling and content problems. Tech-art demands on a game with that scope and that turbulence would have been heavy.
- **Brief stretch on [[Breakaway]]** — the AGS competitive sports/brawler cancelled in 2018. Another cancelled-project entry.
- **Three steps from Bezos on the org chart at one point.** Visible to the upper reaches of one of the largest tech companies on Earth — even if the visibility was structural, decisions about Paul's work were being reviewed at a level most ICs never see.

## What this chapter taught Paul

Different lesson from [[Blizzard Entertainment]] and very different from the post-Amazon turbulent stretch. **The infrastructure-platform-tech-giant model.** Different culture, different epistemic standards, different decision-making processes, different definitions of success than Blizzard's prestige-studio model.

**Comparative-cultures fact**: Paul has now lived inside a *prestige creative-led* model (Blizzard) and a *infrastructure-platform-tech-giant* model (Amazon), and shortly after entered *founder-led startup* mode at [[Sentient Machines]]. Three modes, comparable directly. That's the same comparative-religion-trained instinct expressed at the corporate-culture level — *multiple coherent ways to organize a games studio, each illuminating different things, none absolute*.

## The formation layer at Amazon (named)

Beyond production work, Amazon was a substantial *learning chapter* — paired with [[Blizzard Entertainment]] in the [[dual-training-convergence]] arc. Where Blizzard's formation layer was *visual-craft-and-classical-painting-pedagogy*, Amazon's was *software-engineering-and-ML-formation-and-enterprise-AI-architecture*:

- **[[Chris Adams]]** — Python, systems composition, design patterns. The first tech artist Paul cites as a direct influence. Software-engineering disciplines applied to tech-art domains.
- **[[Olcay Boz]]** — formal ML instructor (loomed large). AWS-flavored production-ML pedagogy.
- **[[Ali Arsanjani]]** — formal ML instructor (loomed large). Enterprise-AI architecture, strategic-and-architectural pedagogy.
- *Other unnamed instructors* loomed less large but contributed.
- **[[First patent application at Amazon]]** — filed, not granted; the formative experience was in the discipline of articulating a technical contribution precisely enough to file.

Together this layer is the second half of Paul's [[dual-training-convergence]]. The current [[Sentient Machines pipeline|sim2real synthetic data work]] sits at the convergence of the Blizzard visual-craft formation and the Amazon software-and-ML formation; neither chapter alone would have made the current work possible.

The [[high-trust-relationship-architecture]] concept predicts there are likely more named figures from Amazon beyond [[Joey Ray Hall]] and the named instructors — peers, leads, ADs Paul actually worked with under him on [[New World]] or in marketing. Status: still partially open.
