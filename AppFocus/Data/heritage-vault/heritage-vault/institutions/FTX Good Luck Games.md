# FTX / Good Luck Games

The first chapter after [[Amazon Game Studios]]. Good Luck Games was the studio FTX acquired in mid-2022 to build *Storybook Brawl*, a fantasy auto-battler.

**Why Paul was here**: he followed [[Jeremy Cranford]]. This is the critical context — Good Luck Games wasn't a random speculative bet on crypto gaming; it was a high-trust personal continuity decision following a long-term collaborator from the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] years.

**How it ended**: FTX collapsed spectacularly in November 2022 — Sam Bankman-Fried, the bankruptcy, the criminal trial. Good Luck Games went down with it or shortly after. **External blast radius event** — the studio itself was functional; the parent company's fraud took it out. That's a specific kind of professional experience: *watching a parent company's fraud destroy an otherwise functional studio*, with no fault on the studio side and no warning available to people doing the actual work.

**The lesson Paul absorbed at this chapter** is different from a project cancellation or a studio's own failure. It's about *due diligence on who you're working for*, not just what you're working on. People who've lived through that have a specific calibration about parent-company integrity that doesn't come from any other kind of cancellation experience.
