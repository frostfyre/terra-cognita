# Impossible

Stealth-mode VR startup founded and run by [[Maxime C Peroumal]]. Based in France. Paul has had a contracting relationship with Impossible across multiple work phases.

## Timeline and function

**Concurrent with [[Hypixel Studios]]**, then **sustaining Paul after Hypixel collapsed/transformed.** The Impossible contracting relationship provided a financial bridge across the Hypixel chapter ending — the same way the [[Amazon Game Studios|Amazon]] chapter's stability had followed the post-Blizzard disruption. The contractor configuration Paul had been building toward is what made the bridge possible; an employee without existing contracting relationships would have had no bridge.

## Work phases

**Phase 1 — [[Radiant Images 4D capture collaboration|4D capture and physical pipeline work]]**: Paul and Maxime collaborated with [[Michael Mansouri]] at Radiant Images on the 4D capture volume project that seeded the portable volumetric studio concept.

**Phase 2 — UE5.x and Metahuman-based virtual volumetric capture**: More recent work, building pipelines at the seam between physical human capture and synthetic Metahuman parametric representation. **The physical/synthetic seam in direct form** — bridging Epic's Metahuman digital-human system with volumetric capture workflows. This work ended fairly abruptly just prior to Paul gaining [[Sentient Machines]] as a client.

The UE5/Metahuman work is directly adjacent to the Sentient Machines synthetic data work — different tools and different downstream consumer, but the same fundamental question: *how do you produce high-quality photorealistic human data for downstream model consumption?* The Impossible work was probably part of what made Paul legible to Harry as a hire.

## The calibrated relationship

The Impossible relationship involves calibrated trust — see [[Maxime C Peroumal]] node for the cost-underestimation pattern and the persona/mystique dimension. Paul values and maintains the relationship; he manages the known patterns actively rather than ignoring them.

## Current status

Maxime is running Impossible in stealth mode. Paul sent a reconnection email proposing a small monthly retainer arrangement — the most recent contact point noted. Status of that proposal and current relationship state: open.
