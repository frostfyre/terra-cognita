# 3DO Company

Game console manufacturer and developer, early-to-mid 1990s. Paul worked here after [[Sony San Diego]], in the period when he imported the "technical artist" title from film — the first time the role was formally named. [[Travis Johnson]] also worked here. The 3DO console was ahead of its time technically but lost the console wars to PlayStation and Saturn.
