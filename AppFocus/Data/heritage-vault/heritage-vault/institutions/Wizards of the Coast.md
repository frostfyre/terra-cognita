# Wizards of the Coast

Trading-card-game and tabletop-RPG publisher. Heritage relevance to Paul is primarily **as the publisher of [[Magic The Gathering|Magic: The Gathering]]** during his obsessive Sony-era period — and as the institution where [[Jeremy Cranford]] held an Art Director chair before joining [[Blizzard Entertainment]].

The Magic AD chair is one of the most influential art-direction posts in modern fantasy illustration. Decisions made here have shaped *the working economy of fantasy painting* for decades by determining who gets commissioned, what aesthetic registers get amplified, and how the genre's visual vocabulary evolves.

**Paul's relationship to WotC is indirect but substantial**: he was a heavy player and aesthetic absorber of Magic during a period when Jeremy Cranford was shaping the art direction. He never worked at WotC himself; the institutional connection to his career runs entirely through Jeremy.
