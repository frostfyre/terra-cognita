# Frostfyre Faerie Glen

Paul's art / craft booth — fairy / fantasy-themed work including figurines (notably **Jackalope Junction**), prints, sculptures, and jewelry. Booth presence at art conventions; table and hotel costs are real benchmarks.

**Not a solo hobby; a shared creative venture between Paul and [[Chandra]].** The map has been treating Frostfyre as Paul's node; the accurate picture is a shared creative enterprise where both their formations converge:

- **Paul's visual lineage** — De Lint, the Pinis, [[Moebius]], Toppi, Wendling, the whole contemporary-mythic register; the tech-art and procedural pipeline background; the bar-setters across the painting tradition
- **Chandra's formation** — belly dance performance background (embodied cultural tradition, disciplined practice); the Sedona family roots; the warmth and care orientation from years of nannying; her own creative practice
- **Their shared life** — the cats, the daughter, the Sedona landscape Chandra's family has been in since the 1950s

Frostfyre is where their two formations meet in a creative practice. **The booth at conventions is both of theirs.**

- The **contemporary mythic / fae-in-the-modern-world** sensibility of [[Charles De Lint]], [[The Pinis]], and the visual side via [[JB Monge]] and Brian Froud / Arthur Rackham / Charles Vess.
- The **independent painter-author career** modeled by [[Brom]] (and to some extent [[Alex Horley]]) — running your own IP, controlling the work.
- The **hand-and-line / [[visual-author-loyalty|visual-author-loyalty]]** instinct — Paul as the brand because the hand is the work.
- The **[[role-authoring]]** pattern — Frostfyre is the same move in a different domain. Paul didn't enter someone else's craft category; he constructed Frostfyre as the frame.

## Jackalope Junction specifically

**Much of Jackalope Junction has inspirations from *[[Jeff Smith|Bone]]*.** This is the most direct creative-lineage link on the entire heritage map — [[Jeff Smith]]'s specific work documented as a direct influence on Paul's own created work. Function-tag: `direct-creative-lineage-to-own-work`.

The character **Lucius** in Jackalope Junction has a documented dual-source lineage — see [[Lucius confluence]] for the full reading. The short version: Bone's Lucius (community anchor, grounded moral center) and Rothfuss's Innkeeper ([[Patrick Rothfuss|*The Name of the Wind*]] — hidden competence compressed into ordinary service) together produce Jackalope Junction's Lucius.

*Bone* contributes a register the De Lint / Pini axis doesn't fully capture: **accessible surface with mythological depth emerging gradually from underneath comedy**. The Pinis and De Lint are earnest-and-operatic; Smith is comic-and-accessible with serious stakes underneath. Jackalope Junction draws on both registers simultaneously.

The *Bone* lineage also traces back through [[Walt Kelly and Pogo|Walt Kelly]] and [[Winsor McCay]] — the American cartooning tradition's found-family-of-misfits register. **The full lineage for Jackalope Junction's tone**: McCay → Kelly → Smith → *Bone* → Jackalope Junction.

The fact that Frostfyre is a *serious* venture rather than a hobby is partly downstream of the [[Blizzard library and academy]] period — Paul deliberately kept the painterly / illustrative side of his formation alive while doing technical work, refusing the standard tech-art drift toward pure-engineering identity.
