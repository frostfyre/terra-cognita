# SIGGRAPH

Attended **annually from 1995 until the early 2010s**, when it "became about hybrid approaches" — AI/ML braided into every part of every pipeline. See [[SIGGRAPH disengagement]] and [[methodological-purist-anchor]].

The 1995–2010s window is, arguably, SIGGRAPH's golden era for the kind of work Paul does. That period produced:

- The maturation of physically-based rendering (Cook-Torrance to Veach's path tracing thesis to early PBR consolidation)
- The Pixar / ILM / Weta production-paper era — RenderMan internals, subsurface scattering, hair and cloth, fluids
- Stam's stable fluids (1999) and the rise of practical fluid simulation in production
- The OpenVDB / level set / SDF work (Museth's papers, the DreamWorks / DigiPro lineage)
- Houdini's ascent from niche to dominant procedural tool, and the SideFX presence at the show
- Procedural texture and shading (Perlin, Worley, the noise lineage that runs into everything Paul does)
- The early GPU-compute era, before it became a marketing category

**Paul wasn't sampling — he was present for the formation of the entire technical foundation his current work rests on.** Heritage entry as significant as the seven [[Blizzard Entertainment|Blizzard]] years, and a different kind: not a place worked but a *field grown up alongside, in real time, in person*. The papers seen presented are the papers that built the toolbox in use now.

## Parallel self-directed study

SIGGRAPH attendance ran in parallel with Paul's [[Renderman]] study via [[Blue Moon Render Tools]] and his work on [[PowerAnimator and the SGI era|PowerAnimator and SGI hardware]]. Together these constitute the [[autodidact-foundational-stream]] — Paul's specific interests at SIGGRAPH were *procedural rendering* and *modeling in realtime*, both research-frontier topics in the 1995–early 2010s window that became professionally relevant to the broader tech-art trade only later.

**SIGGRAPH wasn't a standalone heritage entry; it was the field-frontier exposure that made the rest of the autodidact stream coherent.** Without the annual paper presentations and tutorials, the Renderman / BMRT self-study would have been a closed loop; with SIGGRAPH attendance, it was a living engagement with the discipline as the discipline itself was forming.
