# Radiant Images

Los Angeles-based high-end camera rental and technology company at the forefront of virtual production, volumetric capture, and emerging imaging technology. Active development partner for productions pushing the boundaries of capture technology — not a passive vendor.

Paul and [[Maxime C Peroumal]] worked with [[Michael Mansouri]] here on the [[Radiant Images 4D capture collaboration|4D capture volume project]] that produced the portable capture studio.

**Sits at the seam between technical imaging and creative production** — exactly the seam-walking territory Paul operates in professionally. The physical-capture-pipeline work here is the complement to the synthetic-data-pipeline work at [[Sentient Machines]]: both are about producing high-quality data for downstream consumption, one from physical reality, one from procedural construction.
