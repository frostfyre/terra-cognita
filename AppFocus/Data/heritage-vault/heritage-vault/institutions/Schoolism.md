# Schoolism

Online school for contemporary visual development, founded by [[Bobby Chiu]]. One of the first serious online illustration education platforms; has shaped a substantial fraction of the working contemporary concept-art and visual-development field.

## Connection to Paul's map

**[[Nathan Fowkes]] teaches at Schoolism** — Paul had direct workshop sessions with Fowkes at [[Blizzard library and academy]]; Schoolism is where Fowkes's teaching practice lives publicly. The two contexts are companions: the Blizzard academy gave Paul direct access to Fowkes in person; Schoolism makes Fowkes's ongoing published teaching available to follow.

Other named figures from Paul's map who teach or have appeared at Schoolism: likely [[Steve Huston]] (whose New Masters Academy work is parallel), possibly others from the [[Lightbox Expo]] community.

## Different function from [[Lightbox Expo]]

Lightbox is the *gathering*; Schoolism is the *school*. Both were Bobby Chiu's projects but serve different functions:

- Schoolism delivers structured, course-form instruction online
- Lightbox provides in-person community, workshops, and lectures

Together they represent Chiu's dual approach to building the contemporary-illustration community's infrastructure: *educational backbone + annual gathering*.
