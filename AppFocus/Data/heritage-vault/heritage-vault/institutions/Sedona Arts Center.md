# Sedona Arts Center

Community arts institution in Sedona, Arizona — Paul's home. The first named **local institution** on the heritage map, distinct from the major studio and convention institutions that dominate the professional timeline.

**Hosted the [[Sullivan-Bluth Sedona workshop]]** during Paul's Sony years (mid-1990s) — when Paul was visiting or passing through Sedona, not yet living there. Full-time Sedona residence came decades later. The workshop is one of Paul's earliest known connections to Sedona — a place he encountered during the Sony years and eventually chose as home.

The Arts Center as a node connects an early passing encounter with the place to Paul's eventual settled presence there. The [[frisson-between-extremes]] somatic recognition of Sedona as home-landscape may have had early roots in visits like this one.
