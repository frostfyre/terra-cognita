# Hypixel Studios

Developer of *Hytale* — a long-anticipated Minecraft-adjacent game in extended development. A division of Riot Games (acquired 2020). Part of Paul's post-Amazon turbulent stretch, though in a different category from the Web3 chapters ([[FTX Good Luck Games]], [[Flickplay]], [[Gala Games]]) — this is a long-development AAA-adjacent project at a Riot subsidiary with its own kind of turbulence around scope and timeline.

The studio has experienced significant restructuring visible in public reporting around 2024–2025.
