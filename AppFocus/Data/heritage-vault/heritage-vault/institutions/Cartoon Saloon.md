# Cartoon Saloon

Irish animation studio based in Kilkenny, Republic of Ireland. Founded 1999 by Tomm Moore, Nora Twomey, and Paul Young.

**The trilogy:** *The Secret of Kells* (2009), *Song of the Sea* (2014), *Wolfwalkers* (2020). A coherent twenty-year body of work, each film drawing on a different strand of Irish mythology and folklore — the illuminated manuscript tradition, the selkie legend, the wolf-woman shapeshifter myth.

## What makes Cartoon Saloon specific

**Deliberately flat, decorative visual style descended from the illuminated manuscript tradition** — the Book of Kells itself, the Lindisfarne Gospels, Celtic interlace. Not trying to be Disney or Pixar; trying to be what Irish visual culture actually *is*. That's a real aesthetic commitment and it's rare in contemporary animation.

The visual language connects to [[Sergio Toppi]]'s decorated-surface approach in comics — both treat the flat surface as primary, both build images through pattern and density rather than through illusionistic depth. Different medium, same aesthetic instinct about what images are for.

## Position in the contemporary-mythic cluster

Cartoon Saloon is the **contemporary animated expression** of the same tradition feeding the whole contemporary-mythic cluster on Paul's map:

- [[WB Yeats]] established it in literature (Celtic revival)
- [[Charles De Lint]] carries it in urban fantasy
- [[JB Monge]] carries it in painting
- [[The Pinis]] carry it in comics
- **Cartoon Saloon carries it in animation**

Different medium, same deep root: the Irish/Celtic fae-in-the-natural-world tradition, the landscape as a living mythological presence rather than a backdrop.

## Connection to [[Northern Ireland and the Celtic tradition]]

Paul's direct experience of the Irish landscape — weeks based in Derry, visits to the Giant's Causeway and Dark Hedges — is the physical substrate that Cartoon Saloon's work animates. The landscape Paul recognized somatically as home-in-contrast (the Derry/Elmira rhyme) is the same landscape these films treat as mythologically alive.
