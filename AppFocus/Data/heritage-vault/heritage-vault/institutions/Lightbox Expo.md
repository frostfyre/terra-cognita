# Lightbox Expo

Working-illustrator convention founded by [[Bobby Chiu]]. Different in DNA from [[San Diego Comic-Con|SDCC]] (pop-culture-mass-market) and [[SIGGRAPH]] (technical-graphics). Closest in spirit to the old [[Spectrum Fantastic Art]] gathering that the [[Spectrum Fantastic road trip]] visited, but with a stronger education and community-building orientation.

## Function as bridge node

Lightbox Expo is one of the primary gathering points for the contemporary-fantasy-and-concept-art illustration community Paul has been in relationship with since the Blizzard years. Adding it as an explicit node reveals connections between figures that were previously implicit:

- **[[Chris Sanders]]** — recurring attendee; the only external figure Paul saw at both SDCC *and* [[SIGGRAPH]] in the same week, and also at Lightbox. High-connectivity bridge figure.
- **[[Claire Wendling]]** — has taught and attended. Significant artistic influence on Paul.
- **[[Nathan Fowkes]]** — regularly present; also teaches at [[Schoolism]] (Chiu's companion online school).
- Likely others already on the map: [[Steve Huston]], [[James Gurney]], [[Donato Giancola]], [[Brom]] — all are the kind of figures who regularly appear at serious illustration gatherings of this type.

## Why this institution node matters more than it looks

Before this node was added, **[[Chris Sanders]] was the only map connection to Lightbox Expo**, which made Lightbox look like a peripheral entry. With Lightbox Expo as a real node, several already-mapped figures connect through it, and the contemporary-illustration cluster becomes more accurately represented as a *community* rather than as a collection of separate influences.

**This is the map-making insight from Obsidian/Gephi already working:** seeing that Sanders was the only Lightbox connection was what surfaced that the institution needed its own node.
