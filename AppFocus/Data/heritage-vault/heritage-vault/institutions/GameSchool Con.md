# GameSchool Con

Gaming convention community with educational and family orientation — at the intersection of tabletop gaming, hobby gaming, and home education communities. [[Chandra]] got Paul and herself involved. Name confirmed.

## Why this fits the map

The community sits at the intersection of two things both Paul and Chandra bring:

- **Paul's tabletop gaming heritage** — D&D, Gamma World, Cyberpunk, [[Dragon Magazine]], the whole formative gaming layer already on the map
- **Chandra's child-care and family orientation** — years of nannying, the warmth and care register, the educational sensibility

Homeschooling and unschooling communities often use games as educational tools; the conventions serving them tend to be warm, family-oriented, craft-inclusive spaces. Adjacent to [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen]]'s convention culture — the kind of community where Frostfyre's work is at home. **Both Paul and Chandra have something that belongs there simultaneously**, which makes it genuinely theirs together rather than primarily one person's community that the other attends.
