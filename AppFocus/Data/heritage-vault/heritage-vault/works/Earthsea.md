# Earthsea

The Wizard of Earthsea (1968) and the subsequent series. Magic as language, naming as power, the anthropological-seriousness instinct applied to fantasy. The school for wizards predates Hogwarts by decades and is doing something philosophically different.

by:: [[Ursula K Le Guin]]
