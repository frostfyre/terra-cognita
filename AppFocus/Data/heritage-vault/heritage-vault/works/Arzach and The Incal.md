# Arzach and The Incal

Arzach (1975) — wordless comic in [[Metal Hurlant Heavy Metal]]. The Incal (1981-1988, with Jodorowsky). The Airtight Garage of Jerry Cornelius (1976-1979). Moebius's defining works: the rendered image carries the worldbuilding; visual specificity IS the world, not decoration on top of it. Also concept art for Alien (1979), Tron (1982), The Fifth Element (1997). Influence on Paul filtered through being a tech artist — Moebius taught Paul what an image is supposed to do, not how to draw.
