# Robert McGinnis covers

McGinnis's paperback covers and movie posters — James Bond posters (Thunderball, Barbarella, several others), thousands of pulp/mystery paperback covers. Fashion illustration elegance applied to genre subjects. The elegant elongation from fashion illustration merged with classical academic figure painting. Lineage node for the noir-pulp visual tradition adjacent to [[Glen Orbik]].
