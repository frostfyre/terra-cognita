# The Summer Dragon novel

The Summer Dragon (2016) — Todd Lockwood's debut novel. Also defining D&D 3rd edition illustration for [[Wizards of the Coast]]. The [[visual-author-loyalty]] function extended to prose — anatomically considered fantasy art where the bodies and creatures feel structurally plausible.
