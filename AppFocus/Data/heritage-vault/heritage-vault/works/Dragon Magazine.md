# Dragon Magazine

Official D&D magazine published by TSR/Wizards. Paul would splurge for issues in the bridge years. [[Phil Foglio]] ran *What's New?* as a regular comic strip inside it. Formation resource for both gaming mechanics and for comics-adjacent art and humor during the tabletop gaming formation years.
