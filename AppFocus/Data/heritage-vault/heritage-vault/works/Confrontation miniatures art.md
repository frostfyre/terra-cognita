# Confrontation miniatures art

Paul Bonner's defining fantasy illustration work for Rackham's Confrontation miniatures game — orcs, goblins, trolls rendered with a warmth and personality that made traditionally 'evil' fantasy races feel like genuine cultures. Also work for Games Workshop. Scandinavian-inflected fantasy illustration tradition. Connected to [[Lightbox Expo]].

by:: [[Paul Bonner]]
