# Swamp Thing and Frankenstein

Wrightson co-created Swamp Thing for DC Comics (1971) and later produced a landmark illustrated edition of Mary Shelley's Frankenstein (1983) — 50 pen-and-ink plates over 7 years. EC Comics horror lineage, [[Heavy Metal magazine]] contributor. The Frankenstein illustrations are considered among the finest pen-and-ink work in American illustration.

by:: [[Bernie Wrightson]]
