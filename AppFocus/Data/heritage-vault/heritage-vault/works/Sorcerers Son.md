# Sorcerer's Son (Phyllis Eisenstein, 1979)

The book Paul names as the bridge from comics into prose fantasy. *"Probably 'Sorcerer's Son,' author forgotten."* Author was Phyllis Eisenstein.

A meaningful bridge book — quieter, more character-interior fantasy than the Brooks/Eddings commercial wave dominating the shelves at the moment. Eisenstein was a writer's writer, well-regarded but never a household name; that Paul found her rather than *Sword of Shannara* says **the bridge wasn't from a bestseller list, it was from browsing or recommendation**.

The "Stranger Things era" framing puts this roughly 1983–1986, dead center for the [[D&D]] + library + bookstore + comics convergence in Paul's life.
