# Sharaz-De

Sharaz-De: Tales from the Thousand and One Nights (1979-2005) — Toppi's magnum opus. Also work for Corriere dei Ragazzi and Il Corriere della Sera. A third European-comics tradition distinct from both the Franco-Belgian ligne claire and the Heavy Metal psychedelic register. The page as decorated surface; the image as information delivery at maximum density. Introduced to Paul via [[Tim Gula]]'s recommendation.

by:: [[Sergio Toppi]]
