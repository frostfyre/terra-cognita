# Campbells Soup Cans

Warhol's Campbell's Soup Cans (1962) — 32 canvases, one for each variety. The [[commitment-to-the-bit]] disposition at fine-art scale: take the premise seriously, don't wink, run until undeniable. Also Interview Magazine (1969-2018), which Warhol founded. The artistic practice and the publication practice as two expressions of the same commitment.
