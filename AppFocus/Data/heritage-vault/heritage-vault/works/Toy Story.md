# Toy Story

Toy Story (1995) — the first fully CG animated feature film. Lasseter directed after being fired from Disney. Also A Bug's Life (1998), Toy Story 2 (1999), Cars (2006). Pixar's founding director. Connected to [[SIGGRAPH]] (the Lasseter demo tradition), the Disney-to-Pixar exodus that created the modern animation industry. Full-arc note: Lasseter departed Pixar/Disney in 2017 amid misconduct allegations.
