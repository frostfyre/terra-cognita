# The Celtic Twilight

The Celtic Twilight (1893), The Wind Among the Reeds (1899), The Tower (1928). Literary and cultural infrastructure for the Irish Revival. Ambient influence — 'Yeats is ambient' — absorbed through the [[Northern Ireland and the Celtic tradition]] layer rather than direct study. Paul didn't seek Yeats out; Yeats was in the air.
