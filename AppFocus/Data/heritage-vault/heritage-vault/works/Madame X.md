# Madame X

Madame X (Portrait of Madame Pierre Gautreau, 1884) — the painting that scandalized Paris and established Sargent simultaneously. The American-in-Europe impressionist portrait tradition. How to paint loosely without losing structure — the visible brushstroke doing the work of multiple tight strokes. Upstream of the Watts Atelier lineage through the Boston atelier tradition.

by:: [[John Singer Sargent]]
