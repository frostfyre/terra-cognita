# Saturday Evening Post covers

J.C. Leyendecker's Saturday Evening Post covers (1899-1943) — over 300 covers defining the visual look of American commercial illustration for four decades. Classical academic training + graphic design sensibility. The tilted planes of color, the economy of the fashion-illustration hand. Upstream of [[Norman Rockwell]] who succeeded him at the Post.

by:: [[J.C. Leyendecker]]
