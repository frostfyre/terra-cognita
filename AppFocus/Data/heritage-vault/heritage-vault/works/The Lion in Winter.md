# The Lion in Winter

The Lion in Winter (1968) — Hepburn as Eleanor of Aquitaine opposite Peter O'Toole as Henry II. The defining theatrical-intelligence-under-pressure performance. Annual viewing for Paul. Also: Bringing Up Baby (1938), The African Queen (1951), Guess Who's Coming to Dinner (1967). The quality Kate Mulgrew brings to Janeway in Voyager is described as Hepburn-like on the map.
