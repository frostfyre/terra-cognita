# Dark Sun paintings

Gerald Brom's defining body of work — the paintings for TSR's Dark Sun D&D campaign setting (1989-1993). Brom designed the visual identity of Athas from paintings that preceded the writing — the art drove the world design, not the other way around. Gothic-dark-fantasy register distinct from the heroic-bright Frazetta lineage. Also authored illustrated novels: The Plucker, Krampus: The Yule Lord, Slewfoot. Connected to [[Frank Frazetta]] (acknowledged influence), [[Wizards of the Coast]], [[Michael Moorcock]] (painted Moorcock covers).
