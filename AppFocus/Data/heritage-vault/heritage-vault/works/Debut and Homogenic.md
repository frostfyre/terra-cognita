# Debut and Homogenic

Debut (1993), Post (1995), Homogenic (1997), Vespertine (2001) — Björk's solo albums from the post-Sugarcubes period. Multi-decade relationship on Paul's map dating to the Sugarcubes (active 1986-92). Seen at Red Rocks. Each album a distinct sonic world; the [[commitment-to-the-bit]] aesthetic applied to each record's universe. Also Biophilia (2011) — nature/technology intersection.
