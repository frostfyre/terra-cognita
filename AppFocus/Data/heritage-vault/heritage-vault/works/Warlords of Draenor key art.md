# Warlords of Draenor key art

Key art for Blizzard's WoW expansion (2014). Horley was the first non-Blizzard staff member approved to produce art for game packaging. Over 500 illustrations for [[Blizzard Entertainment]] across every franchise. Self-taught Italian painter from Milan; influenced by [[Frank Frazetta]], Corben, Kirby. Also worked for [[Heavy Metal magazine]], Marvel, DC, [[Wizards of the Coast]].
