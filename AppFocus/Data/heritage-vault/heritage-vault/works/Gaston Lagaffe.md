# Gaston Lagaffe

Franquin's signature character (1957-1997) — the lovable incompetent office worker who destroys everything around him through well-meaning cluelessness. Also Spirou et Fantasio (1947-1968). Franquin is the bridge between the Franco-Belgian ligne claire tradition and the more expressive, gestural European cartooning that influenced [[Claire Wendling]] and the broader European comics lineage on the map.
