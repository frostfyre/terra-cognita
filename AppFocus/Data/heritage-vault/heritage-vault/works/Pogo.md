# Pogo

Pogo (1948-1975) — Walt Kelly's political satire comic strip set in the Okefenokee Swamp. Upstream of [[Jeff Smith]]'s Bone (the American-cartooning found-family-in-nature lineage). 'We have met the enemy and he is us' as the single most compressed version of the [[protagonist-as-the-problem]] pattern in American popular culture.
