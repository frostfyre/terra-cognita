# Gormenghast

Trilogy (1946-59). Titus Groan, Gormenghast, Titus Alone. The vast decaying castle as setting and character simultaneously. The book where the architecture is its own character. Maximalist prose, decadent imagery, the place-as-character instinct in its most extreme form.

by:: [[Mervyn Peake]]
