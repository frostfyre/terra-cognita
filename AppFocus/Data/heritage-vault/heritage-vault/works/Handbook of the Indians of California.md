# Handbook of the Indians of California

Alfred Kroeber's anthropological fieldwork and texts — Handbook of the Indians of California (1925) and foundational UC Berkeley anthropology. Upstream cause of [[Ursula K Le Guin]]'s anthropological worldbuilding instinct (Kroeber was Le Guin's father). Also Theodora Kroeber (Le Guin's mother) who wrote Ishi in Two Worlds (1961). The anthropological-seriousness entering fantasy literature through family lineage.

by:: [[Alfred Kroeber]]
