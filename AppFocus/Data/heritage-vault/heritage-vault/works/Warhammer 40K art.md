# Warhammer 40K art

John Blanche's defining artwork for Games Workshop's Warhammer and Warhammer 40K universes. The gothic-grimdark aesthetic that defines 40K's visual identity — the counterweight tradition to American heroic-fantasy painting. Ink-and-wash, distressed surfaces, cathedral-scale decay. Connected to [[Wizards of the Coast]] (parallel fantasy-game illustration tradition via Citadel/GW).
