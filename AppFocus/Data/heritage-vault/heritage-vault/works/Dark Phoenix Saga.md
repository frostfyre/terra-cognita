# Dark Phoenix Saga

Marvel, *Uncanny X-Men* #129–138, 1980. [[Chris Claremont]] and John Byrne. Self-reported as highly influential.

The other foundational Claremont X-Men text alongside *[[God Loves Man Kills]]*. Different functional weight: **operatic tragedy with cosmic stakes and personal cost.** Jean Grey ascending to godlike power, becoming uncontainable, choosing self-destruction to protect those she loves.

**This is the [[Michael Moorcock|Eternal Champion]] / Elric structural pattern transposed into superhero mode.** The protagonist who is the problem. The doomed cycle. The cosmic register held seriously alongside intimate human stakes. Paul absorbed it *on top of* Moorcock as **structural reinforcement of an instinct already there.**

Pairs with [[God Loves Man Kills]] as the two anchors of Paul's Marvel intake — together with the broader Claremont run, the most committed long-arc serialized worldbuilding in mainstream comics.
