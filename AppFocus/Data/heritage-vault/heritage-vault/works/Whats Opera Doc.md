# Whats Opera Doc

What's Opera, Doc? (1957) — Bugs Bunny and Elmer Fudd performing Wagner's Ring cycle in 7 minutes. The [[commitment-to-the-bit]] disposition at its most compressed and perfect. Also Duck Dodgers in the 24½th Century (1953), One Froggy Evening (1955), The Dot and the Line (1965). Jones's specific contribution: timing as meaning, character as philosophical argument, comedy as ethics. The [[operatic-tragic-register]] in cartoon form.

by:: [[Chuck Jones]]
