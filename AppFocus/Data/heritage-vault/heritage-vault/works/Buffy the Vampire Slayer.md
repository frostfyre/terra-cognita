# Buffy the Vampire Slayer

Buffy the Vampire Slayer (1997-2003), Firefly (2002-2003), Dr. Horrible's Sing-Along Blog (2008). The [[found-family-of-misfits]] aesthetic at its most developed in television. Buffy as the monster-fighting outsider who builds family; Firefly as the same pattern in space-western register; Dr. Horrible as the [[operatic-tragic-register]] compressed to 42 minutes. Full-arc note: Whedon's workplace conduct allegations are on the map; the work's influence predates and is distinct from the personal failings.

by:: [[Joss Whedon]]
