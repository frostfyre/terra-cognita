# Tim Gula

**Direct instructor — life drawing sessions** at the [[Blizzard library and academy]] during Paul's seven years there. **Introduced to Paul by [[Glen Orbik]]** — which makes Gula the second link in the chain: Orbik → Gula → [[Sergio Toppi]]. Gula in turn introduced Paul to Toppi's work.

The *foundational* layer underneath everything else in the academy's instructor roster. Life drawing is *the* fundamental practice — the discipline that anchors every other visual skill. Color and light theory ([[James Gurney]], [[Nathan Fowkes]]) sits *on top of* observational drawing fundamentals. And the gesture-and-construction theory ([[Glenn Vilppu]], Sony era) is the framework Gula's sessions kept *practiced* across the Blizzard years.

**For a tech artist to keep up regular life drawing sessions across years is unusual and deliberate.** Most tech artists let that practice die — it's one of the first things to atrophy when the day job is engineering-flavored. Paul didn't, which is consistent with the entire pattern of *deliberately refusing the tech-art drift toward pure-engineering identity* that the [[Blizzard library and academy]] node calls out.

Function for the map: anchoring node for the "Paul kept up the underlying observational discipline" thread. Without Gula's sessions in the picture, the Gurney / Fowkes / Watts upper-funnel teaching would be sitting on top of nothing; with him, the academy's pedagogical layer is *complete* — fundamentals through advanced color-and-light through working-painter exposure.
