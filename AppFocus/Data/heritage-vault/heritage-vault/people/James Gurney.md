# James Gurney

**Direct instructor.** Classes and painting sessions with Paul during the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] years, via the [[Blizzard library and academy]]. Paul still follows his published work.

The most influential working teacher of *plein-air-derived imaginative-realist painting* in the contemporary fantasy field. Author of *Color and Light* and *Imaginative Realism* — books that are how a generation of concept artists and illustrators learned to see. *Dinotopia* is the famous IP; the methodological books are the working artifact.

Gurney's methodology explicitly bridges the 19th-century academic tradition (Sorolla, the Russian Itinerants, the Brandywine school) to invented-world painting. **His core posture: you can paint anything if you observe accurately first.** That is the painting-equivalent of the [[methodological-purist-anchor]] Paul holds in simulation. *Direct precedent in another medium.*

**Function on the map:** the single most consequential entry in the [[Blizzard library and academy]] instructor layer for color-and-light teaching. Together with [[Nathan Fowkes]], gives Paul exposure to both major lineages of contemporary color-and-light teaching (imaginative realist / animation production). Together with [[Tim Gula]]'s life drawing, [[Steve Huston]]'s figure mastery, and [[Bob Watts]]'s atelier curriculum, represents a *real art education embedded in a games-industry tech-art career, deliberately maintained for years* — the explanation of why Paul's eye is what it is.

The "still follow" is its own function-tag — active relationship to a tradition, not a memory of one. Gurney remains in Paul's live attention.
