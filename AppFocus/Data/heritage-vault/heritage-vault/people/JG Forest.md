# JG Forest

Paul's paternal grandfather. Founder of **International Stock Foods (ISF)**, makers of Silo Guard — agricultural feed preservation and grain storage products. Built a global industry from a found recipe.

**Sustained direct relationship** — grandparents lived down the street for most of Paul's formative years. Sunday dinners were a family tradition. This is not family lore; it's a real, ongoing, domestic relationship across childhood. JG Forest was the person at the end of the street, seen weekly, present across the formative period.

**Paid for Paul's Catholic education** alongside his wife — a direct material investment in Paul's formation.

## The formation story

Found an old recipe. Recognized its commercial potential. Built the distribution and commercial infrastructure around it. Scaled it to an international company.

**Not the lone-genius-invents-from-nothing narrative.** The recognition-and-translation intelligence: seeing value in existing material, understanding how to realize that potential in a new context, building something global from that recognition.

## The generational connection

The specific intelligence JG Forest demonstrated — **recognize the valuable thing that already exists, build the infrastructure to realize its potential, scale it** — runs forward through the generations with striking consistency:

Paul doesn't invent Houdini, USD, Newton, or IsaacSim. He recognizes their combinatorial potential for sim2real pipelines and builds the architecture around them. Same move, different domain, different century. The grandfather who commercialized an old recipe and the grandson who builds synthetic data pipelines from existing simulation tools are operating with the same core intelligence.
