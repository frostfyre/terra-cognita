## Calibrated discretion

Paul's description: *"the practical sort who knew when to get involved and knew when to stay away."*

That's high praise of a specific kind. **Calibrated discretion** — the judgment to know when one's involvement adds value versus when it distorts what's already working. Rare and precise quality in senior tech artists, especially leads. Lead TAs often default to one of two failure modes: too involved (micromanaging, bottlenecking) or too absent (leaving teams without architectural guidance). Rudy operating with calibrated discretion across both directions tells us he held the same anti-inflation register Paul does — *don't perform leadership; provide it where it's needed and stay out of where it isn't*.

Same disposition as Paul's *"not co-curator"* correction and *"pretty sad as I recall"* register and tier-honest placement of hair bands. **The disposition is shared, not just the technical chops.**
