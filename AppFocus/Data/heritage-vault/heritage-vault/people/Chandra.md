# Chandra

Paul's wife. The most foundational personal-layer node on the entire heritage map.

## How they met

Met in sophomore year of high school, in **choir**. Dated for a couple of years. Reconnected years later. Married **1995 in Sedona** — the same year as Sony San Diego, the PSX launch, and the beginning of the professional map's first chapter. Two foundational things happening in the same year.

## The root node of the professional map

**Chandra is responsible for Paul applying to Sony San Diego.**

This is the sentence that reorganizes everything. The causal chain from her prompting that application runs through the entire professional heritage map:

Chandra → Sony application → [[Sony San Diego]] → [[John Scharmen]] → [[Blizzard Entertainment]] → the entire Blizzard formation layer ([[Jeremy Cranford]], [[Alex Horley]], [[Mark Gibbons]], [[Blizzard library and academy]], [[Spectrum Fantastic road trip]]) → [[Amazon Game Studios]] → [[Impossible]] / [[Radiant Images]] → [[Sentient Machines]]

Without Chandra's prompting, none of that chain looks the same. **She's not just a personal-layer figure who exists alongside the professional map. She's the root node of it.**

The [[high-trust-relationship-architecture]] concept identifies the named humans who caused each professional chapter. Chandra is the cause of the cause of the cause — the deepest root of that whole structure.

## Early formation — belly dancer professionally

**Professionally a belly dancer in high school** — an unusual and specific detail. Performance in an art form combining athletic discipline, musical embodiment, and cultural tradition (Middle Eastern, North African, Central Asian folk and classical forms), done at a professional level while still a teenager. Same [[fully-present-participant]] disposition Paul holds — showing up all the way to something serious. She was already that person when they met.

The music connection is real: belly dance is music-embodied. She was in choir *and* professionally performing to music simultaneously when they met. Their meeting was in music; her professional work was through music. That's the medium where the most important encounter in Paul's life happened.

**Chandra is also an Elfquest fan — independently, in isolation.** Paul didn't find out until well after marriage.

This is not shared formation (absorbed together) or introduction (one brought it to the other). ## Society for Creative Anachronism

**Chandra got them involved in the SCA in high school.** The SCA is the living-history organization doing medieval recreation — combat, costume, arts and crafts, heraldry, music, cooking, the whole medieval cultural package. In the 1980s–90s it was deeply intertwined with fantasy fandom, folk music, hand-crafts, and the making culture Frostfyre exists inside.

What the SCA adds to the map:

- **Craft and making as serious practice** — armor, fabric, calligraphy, illumination, leatherwork, taught with real rigor. Formation in *making things well with your hands*. The Frostfyre sculptures, crocheted figures, and jewelry have roots here.
- **Living-history worldbuilding** — constructing a persona, researching the period, making the material culture, inhabiting the world. Collaborative worldbuilding through embodied practice. Adjacent to visual worldbuilding; the [[Robert Heinlein|Heinlein]] competence-as-worldbuilding instinct expressed in craft.
- **The Chandra origin** — she got them involved. Another instance of Chandra as the person who opens the door.
