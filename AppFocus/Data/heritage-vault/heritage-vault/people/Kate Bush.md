# Kate Bush

**Direct relationship** — Paul loves Kate Bush. "Running Up That Hill" as the known entry point; the 1985 original from *Hounds of Love* (her masterwork album).

The song's specific quality: wanting to trade places with someone you love to understand them, achieved through a deal with God. **Mythological-confessional-interior register** in exactly the form the [[female-fronted-authorial-project]] cluster recognizes — personal emotional stakes elevated to mythological scale, God as a figure in a deal rather than an authority, the interior world made cosmically large.

## Both lineage node and direct influence

Kate Bush is **upstream of [[Tori Amos]]** (Tori cited her explicitly as a primary influence — the decision to approach piano as a vehicle for personal and mythological material rather than accompaniment). She's also an independent aesthetic relationship for Paul. Both functions are real.

## Position in the cluster

A career of complete artistic control, deep mythological and literary reference (Wuthering Heights, Tennyson, Joyce's *Ulysses*, Biblical imagery), classical piano as structural foundation, refusal to stay in a single aesthetic register across albums. **One of the earliest examples of the female-fronted-authorial-project-with-sustained-control disposition** in post-1970s pop. She was doing it before most of the cluster's other figures were.
