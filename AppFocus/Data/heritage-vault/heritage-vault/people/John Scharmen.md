# John Scharmen

**Lifelong friend.** First worked together at [[Sony San Diego]] during the PlayStation launch, shipped ***[[Spawn The Eternal]]*** on PSX (1997). One of the most load-bearing single relationships across the entire heritage map.

Critical fact: **Scharmen got Paul into [[Blizzard Entertainment]]**, along with [[Steve Aguilar]] — *those two were the only Sony-to-Blizzard transit*. Earlier draft of this node assumed a larger cohort wave; correction confirmed: Scharmen + Aguilar, tight-pair transit. The rest of the [[Sony San Diego animation team]] stayed in Sony-era memory rather than becoming a Blizzard-era cohort. Without Scharmen the Lich King-through-Legion run doesn't happen, and without that run the entire Blizzard heritage layer ([[Alex Horley]], [[Mark Gibbons]], [[John Polidora]], [[Wei Wang]], [[Brom]] encounters, the [[Blizzard library and academy]], the [[Spectrum Fantastic road trip]]) doesn't accumulate. Function-tag: `network-mover` — the people who put your name into rooms you weren't in.

The 1995 PSX-launch chapter is still important — it's where the cohort that *included* Scharmen and Aguilar coalesced — but the active forward-propagation into Blizzard was the two of them.

## Shared formation layers

Beyond the professional relationship, Paul and Scharmen share **at least two named formation layers** that gave them a common sub-vocabulary — both dating to the [[Sony San Diego]] years:

**[[Glenn Vilppu]]** — attended Vilppu sessions together during Sony and after on his independent workshop circuit. The gesture-first, construction-second framework as shared animation-theory language.

**[[Chuck Jones]]** — shared direct formation via the [[Warner Bros VTEL sessions]] at Sony. Jones-vocabulary for timing and character as working shared language.

**[[Masamune Shirow|Ghost in the Shell PS1 game]]** — played together at Sony and invented a competition mode around the single-player Tachikoma shooter. A third shared formation experience from the Sony years: not instruction or workshop but collaborative play that produced a self-designed competitive format. The social fabric of the team through shared gaming — same pattern as [[star-trek-star-wars-orville|X-Wing vs TIE Fighter]] at Sony.

These shared layers are part of why the Scharmen relationship has been as durable as it has. Not just collegial trust, but *overlapping formation that produces working vocabulary in common.* Same pattern as [[Blue Moon Render Tools|BMRT]] producing instant recognition with [[Rudy Cortez]], but predating that by a decade and running through animation-craft rather than rendering-algorithms. Both shared formations are Sony-era — meaning the deepest common vocabulary between Paul and Scharmen was established in the first chapter they shared.

Companion on the [[Spectrum Fantastic road trip]] to Kansas City along with [[John Polidora]] — pilgrimage to the painters' convention. Driving to Spectrum is a tell about what someone takes seriously; Scharmen shares the disposition.
