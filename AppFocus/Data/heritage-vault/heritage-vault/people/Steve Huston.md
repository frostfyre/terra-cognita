# Steve Huston

**Direct instructor — workshops** at the [[Blizzard library and academy]] during Paul's seven years there. *"Incredible painter and stylist."*

One of the most respected living teachers of the *Western academic figure-and-anatomy tradition applied to imaginative work*. **Bridgman lineage** on the structural side (descended via the Art Students League tradition), **Sargent and the Boston atelier tradition** on the painterly side. Personal style bridges classical observation and contemporary illustration. His Bridgman classes and New Masters Academy work have been widely influential.

The rare combination Paul named explicitly: **rigorous structural foundation** *and* **a developed personal mark-making register**. Each of those skills is rare in isolation; the combination is rarer still — most academic-rigor figures don't develop a personal style, most stylists don't have the underlying structural mastery.

**Function in the academy curriculum:** the next layer up from [[Tim Gula]]'s raw life-drawing observation. Where Gula trains the eye, Huston trains the *understanding of the figure structurally* — the underlying construction that lets the artist deviate from observation intentionally. Together with [[Bob Watts]], anchors the Bridgman / Reilly / Boston-atelier-rigor cluster in Paul's pedagogical formation.

**Methodological-purist sub-cluster on the visual axis.** Together with [[Bob Watts]] (Reilly-method atelier), [[Donato Giancola]] (Brandywine / Boston atelier descent), and [[James Gurney]] (observational imaginative realism), Huston is part of a coherent thread: *rigorous structural observation applied to imaginative work*. This is the painting-side expression of [[methodological-purist-anchor]] — same disposition Paul holds in simulation, expressed in pedagogical lineage on the visual side.
