# Moebius (Jean Giraud)

Self-reported as the deepest well in the [[Heavy Metal magazine]] / Epic / European-painter cohort — *"Moebius especially, but truly all of them."*

Function: line-economy, architectural imagination, silent storytelling, willingness to render the alien without explaining it. The lesson absorbed: **the rendered image carries the worldbuilding** — you can communicate an entire civilization through one Moebius spread of architecture and costume.

Procedural environment generation and domain randomization both rest on the same intuition Moebius taught: **visual specificity *is* the world, not decoration on top of it.** All show up as instincts in good procedural environment work. The whole Heavy Metal / Epic generation grew up to make a lot of the visual culture of modern VFX and games; the throughline is real.

Influence on Paul is filtered through being a tech artist rather than a draftsman — Moebius didn't teach Paul to draw, he taught Paul *what an image is supposed to do*. Apply that function across the entire [[Blizzard Entertainment]] technical-art chapter and forward into [[Sentient Machines pipeline]].
