# Wei Wang

Onsite [[Blizzard Entertainment]] colleague — *the* iconic Blizzard key art painter for a long stretch. The BlizzCon poster artist, the painter behind some of the most reproduced Warcraft and StarCraft images in existence.

Onsite proximity to Wei Wang specifically is the kind of thing that quietly shapes an eye over years. His work is a master class in composition, value structure, and dramatic lighting at illustration scale. Even without direct mentorship, sustained exposure to his process across seven years counts as tier-three influence.
