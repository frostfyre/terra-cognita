# Paul Stamets

Mycologist, researcher, author, entrepreneur. **Both his area of research and his personal story are influential** — named together, which is worth marking as a specific kind of influence: the person whose *work* and whose *way of being in the world* are simultaneously interesting.

## The research

Stamets's work on fungal networks — mycelium as the wood-wide-web, mushrooms as bioremediation tools, psilocybin research, the role of fungi in ecosystem health — is systems-thinking applied to biological networks. The mycelium network as a distributed intelligence, the fungal relationship to plant and soil systems as an interlocking-systems problem: this is the [[Frank Herbert|Herbert-style interlocking-systems-ecology]] instinct applied to real science rather than worldbuilding.

The research resonates with Paul's pipeline architecture instinct — distributed systems, emergent behavior from local rules, the network as the functional unit rather than the individual node. The SeedTree hierarchical seed derivation system at [[Sentient Machines]] is not entirely unlike mycelial branching logic: a root generates downstream variation through branching paths.

## The personal story

Stamets's formation story: stutterer who found confidence through psilocybin experience, self-taught mycologist who built expertise through obsessive direct study before mycology was academically respectable. The **autodidact-frontier-scientist** register — doing the work before the institutional context existed to support it. Adjacent to the [[autodidact-foundational-stream]] disposition: Paul studying Renderman before there was a professional context for it; Stamets studying mycelium before it was academically fashionable.

The entrepreneurial dimension (Fungi Perfecti, the various patents and products) connects to the role-authoring-at-commercial-scale pattern — building the institution because the existing ones don't serve the work.

## The influence type

*Both research and story* — this is the same dual-influence pattern as [[Edgar Rice Burroughs]] (story more than books) but more balanced. With ERB the personal story dominated; with Stamets the research and the story carry approximately equal weight. Worth marking as a specific category: **the figure whose formation story and whose intellectual output are both directly interesting**, rather than one or the other.
