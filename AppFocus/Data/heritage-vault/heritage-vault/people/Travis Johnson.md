# Travis Johnson

Professional peer across two studio chapters: [[3DO]] and [[Secret Level SEGA]]. Recently of Probably Monsters (Harold Ryan's humane-studio-culture holding company). One of the longer-running professional connections on the map — not a single chapter but sustained presence across two distinct institutional contexts.

## The two-chapter connection

**[[3DO]]** — where Paul imported the "technical artist" title from film. Travis was there in the same period.

**[[Secret Level SEGA]]** — *Golden Axe*, *Iron Man*. Travis was there too.

Two chapters, same person across them. That kind of sustained professional co-presence across multiple studios is a real form of relationship — not just colleagues in one place but people whose careers have run in parallel through multiple contexts.

## "A bit annoyed at me for becoming a director"

Worth holding precisely as written — not a rupture, not a serious falling-out. *A bit annoyed.* A real long-term professional relationship where competition for roles and recognition is real but doesn't override the fundamental connection. Paul naming it accurately rather than smoothing it over is the anti-inflation register operating in the professional-personal layer.

This is also an honest view of the [[role-authoring]] pattern from an outside perspective. Paul writing his own job description and landing the director role was, from Travis's position, a colleague getting something Travis may have wanted. The role-authoring disposition produces outcomes that create friction with peers. Both the disposition and the friction are real; the relationship survived both.

## Shared operational vocabulary

*"Shared fondness for solving hard problems and ludicrous work ethic."*

The [[shared-formation-produces-recognition]] pattern operating through *disposition* rather than aesthetic formation. Not a shared aesthetic sub-vocabulary (though Elfquest gives that too) but a shared **operational** sub-vocabulary: two people who both sprint toward hard problems rather than away from them can skip negotiation about work culture. The *ludicrous* qualifier is self-aware — both of them know the quantity and intensity they bring is beyond what the situation typically requires, and it's a point of mutual recognition rather than a problem.
