# J.K. Rowling / Harry Potter

Both the books and films. Absorbed young-but-not-child — *"not quite young, but not old"* when the series landed. The full seven-book arc plus the film adaptations.

## The formation story resonance

*"The homeless to success story of her formation resonates with me, though obviously not at the same scale."*

Rowling was a single mother on welfare, writing in Edinburgh cafés during her daughter's nap times, rejected by multiple publishers before Bloomsbury took the first book. The specific resonance: **the person who builds the thing from nothing, against institutional indifference or rejection, through sheer commitment to the work.** Same family as [[Joan Jett]] (Blackheart Records after label rejections), [[Edgar Rice Burroughs]] (first Tarzan at forty after multiple career failures), [[Jeff Smith]] (self-published Bone for its entire original run).

Paul's framing — *"obviously not at the same scale"* — is the anti-inflation register operating correctly. The resonance is with the *structure* of the story (built from nothing, against resistance) not with the scale of the outcome. That's honest.

## The work itself

The Harry Potter series as literature: the found-family-of-misfits at Hogwarts, the long-arc consequence worldbuilding across seven volumes, the progressive darkening from children's adventure to genuine tragedy. The [[operatic-tragic-register]] arriving in the final volumes — Dumbledore's arc, Snape's arc, the [[protagonist-as-the-problem]] dimension (Harry as a Horcrux, the prophesied death).

## The full arc — Rowling's later political stances

Rowling's subsequent and sustained public opposition to trans rights has been well-documented and is real. The map holds this accurately — the work Paul absorbed and the person's later stances are both on the map, neither canceling the other. Same framing as Lasseter, Gaiman, Whedon.
