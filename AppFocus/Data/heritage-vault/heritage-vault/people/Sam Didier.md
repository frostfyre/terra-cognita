# Sam Didier (Samwise)

Senior Art Director and co-founder at [[Blizzard Entertainment]]. The visual identity of Blizzard's house style — the bold, readable, high-saturation heroic-fantasy aesthetic — is substantially Samwise's creation. Paul worked there during his tenure.
