# Charlie N. Holmberg

*The Paper Magician* series and many others. Prolific indie-published / small-press fantasy author. **Quite a bit** of her work in Paul's reading — not comprehensive like Kingfisher but sustained.

## The register

Clean, accessible fantasy with **magic systems that have internal logic and cost** — the Sanderson-adjacent approach (magic as a system with rules, consequences, and limits) in a warmer, less epic register. Female protagonists with genuine agency. Emotional stakes that are real without being operatically dark.

The "warm stakes" phrasing captures the specific tonal position: the threat is real, the protagonist is genuinely at risk, but the overall register is more inviting than grimdark. The [[accessible-surface-with-real-stakes]] structure operated at a lower intensity level than Carriger's wit or Kingfisher's horror — a different point on the same axis.

## The indie-publishing note

Holmberg built her career through indie publishing and small press before broader distribution — another instance of the self-publishing / built-the-thing-myself pattern that runs across Paul's reading (Jeff Smith's Bone, Rothfuss's independent path, Bell's supernatural western). The pattern keeps appearing: Paul's reading tends toward authors who maintained control of their work.
