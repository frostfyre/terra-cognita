# Bernie Wrightson

**Lineage node** — upstream of [[Brom]]'s horror-gothic register.

Wrightson (1948–2017) was an American comics artist and illustrator best known for co-creating *Swamp Thing* (1971) and for his illustrated edition of Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein* (1983) — the latter widely considered one of the great achievements in comics-adjacent illustration of the 20th century, featuring pen-and-ink illustrations of extraordinary density and horror. He was also a founding member of the original *Heavy Metal* American circle.

His tradition: **EC Comics horror** (the 1950s *Tales from the Crypt* / *Vault of Horror* aesthetic) filtered through a classical pen-and-ink draftsmanship that goes back to the 19th-century wood-engraving and etching traditions (Gustave Doré is the deep ancestor). Dense cross-hatching, extreme value contrast, grotesque figure work, horror as a serious aesthetic category rather than a commercial genre.

[[Brom]] absorbed this tradition and moved it into oil paint — the same horror-gothic sensibility, the same comfort with ugliness and darkness as valid aesthetic registers, translated into a more contemporary painterly mode.
