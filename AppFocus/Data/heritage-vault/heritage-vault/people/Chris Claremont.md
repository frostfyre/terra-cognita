# Chris Claremont

The author behind both anchors of Paul's Marvel intake: ***God Loves, Man Kills*** and the ***Dark Phoenix Saga***. Sixteen years of continuous X-Men writing, hundreds of issues, an enormous cast accumulating relationships and grief and loyalty.

The Claremont X-Men is **the most sustained worldbuilding-with-found-family-of-misfits text in mainstream comics history** — paired with [[The Pinis|Elfquest]] as the two most committed long-arc serialized worldbuilds Paul absorbed. Two of his most-cited comics influences are the medium's two most committed long-arc projects of the era. Not coincidence.

Function-tags:

- ***God Loves, Man Kills*** (1982, with Brent Anderson) — the X-Men as persecuted minority, religious fundamentalism as antagonist, Magneto and Xavier in dialogue rather than combat. Reinforces academically what the [[comparative religion class]] was teaching: *orthodoxies can be the problem*, institutional certainty is dangerous, minorities-as-mutants is a workable lens for thinking about real social structures.
- ***Dark Phoenix Saga*** (1980, with John Byrne) — operatic tragedy with cosmic stakes and personal cost. The protagonist who is the problem. The doomed cycle. **The Eternal Champion / [[Michael Moorcock|Elric]] structural pattern transposed into superhero mode.** Absorbed *on top of* Moorcock as structural reinforcement of an instinct already there.
