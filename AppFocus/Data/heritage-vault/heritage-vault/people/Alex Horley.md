# Alex Horley

Friend and colleague. Met at [[Blizzard Entertainment]] during the WoW years; the friendship has persisted past that chapter (kept-in-touch).

Serious painter with deep [[Heavy Metal magazine|Heavy Metal]] / [[Frank Frazetta|Frazetta]] / [[Brom]] DNA, along with a significant **DC Comics painters** lineage — Brian Bolland, Bill Sienkiewicz, Dave McKean, and the broader DC/Vertigo painted-cover tradition of the 1980s–90s. [[Simon Bisley]] also named as an influence, connecting the 2000 AD/Heavy Metal British-painterly stream to the DC side. Both streams converge in the same register: painterly, heavy, illustration-serious.

Together with [[Mark Gibbons]] gives Paul **two living, working, accessible relationships in the high-end fantasy painting world** — from different lineages (Horley more Heavy Metal / Frazetta-painterly, Gibbons more Citadel / [[John Blanche]] gothic). Most people who *like* this tradition have no relationship to anyone working in it. Paul has two. That's a fact about his actual position in the ecosystem, not just his influences.

Function for Paul: not stylistic influence (Paul is a tech artist, not a painter) — *living transmission of a tradition Paul takes seriously*. The Frostfyre work, the procedural pipeline aesthetics, the way Paul talks about professional positioning — all carry the weight of someone who has watched serious painters work and treats craft as a real category.
