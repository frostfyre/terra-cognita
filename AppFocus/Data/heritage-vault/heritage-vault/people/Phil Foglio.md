# Phil Foglio

Comics-side cousin to [[Robert Asprin]] and [[Terry Pratchett]] in the comic-fantasy register. Brings the *gamer-craftsman-humorist* sensibility: humor, craftsmanship, deep genre literacy, gaming-adjacent (he was doing *What's New?* in [[Dragon Magazine]] in the bridge years — that's the issues Paul was splurging for), eventually *Girl Genius*.

Genre worn lightly, mechanics taken seriously, jokes that respect the reader's intelligence. Rhymes forward with the prose preferences ("Same word. Different discipline.") and with [[Mark Twain]] — comedy that respects its reader.
