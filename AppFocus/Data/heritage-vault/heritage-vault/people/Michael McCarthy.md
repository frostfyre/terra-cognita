# Michael McCarthy

Lead designer at [[Gala Games]]. The person who specifically cited [[Patrick Rothfuss]]'s books as the inspiration for the game world's mechanics, sending Paul to Rothfuss — which eventually fed into [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen|Jackalope Junction]]'s Lucius character via the [[Lucius confluence]].

## What his citation tells us about his design approach

A lead designer who cites Rothfuss as foundational to world mechanics is doing something specific — treating *The Name of the Wind*'s Sympathy system and the Arcanum's internal logic as a *design document*. That's a particular kind of designer: someone who thinks about game mechanics through the lens of literary world-systems, the [[Frank Herbert|Herbert-style interlocking-systems]] approach applied to game design. The working culture at Gala, at least in his corner of it, was operating at that level of conceptual ambition.
