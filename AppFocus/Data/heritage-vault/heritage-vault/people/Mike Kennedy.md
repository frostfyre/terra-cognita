# Mike Kennedy

Colleague at [[3DO]] (~1998) and [[Namco]] (~2004–2005). **Two separate studio chapters**, which means the relationship persisted across a multi-year gap between those chapters — same durable-across-studios quality as [[John Scharmen]] and [[Jeremy Cranford]], expressed in a different register. Still connected on LinkedIn and Facebook: low-bandwidth sustained contact.

Went on to found **Magnetic Press** out of Chicago — an independent comics and graphic novel publisher known for high-production-quality art books and translated European comics (franco-Belgian and international tradition).

## The framed Spielberg rejection letter

Paul's detail: Mike had a framed rejection letter from Spielberg.

**A framed rejection letter isn't a keepsake of failure — it's a declaration of seriousness.** You frame something when it matters, when it marks an attempt that was real, when you want it visible rather than filed. A rejection from Spielberg means he was trying to get a project in front of one of the most powerful creative figures in film — swinging at a real target, not playing it safe. The framing says: *I tried this, it didn't work, I'm not hiding that, I'm marking it as part of my story.*

That's a specific and unusual posture. It connects to the [[fully-present-participant]] disposition's refusal-edge — *show up all the way, including to the attempt that doesn't work, and don't perform shame about the outcome.* The framed letter is an anti-fragility artifact: evidence of risk-taking worn publicly rather than hidden. Paul noticed it and remembered it, which means it registered as something about *who Kennedy was*, not just a funny anecdote.

## Magnetic Press as [[role-authoring]] at institutional scale

The career arc: games industry (3DO, Namco) → founded an independent publishing house for high-quality European and international comics.

The Spielberg rejection is part of this arc — he tried the major-studio route, got turned down, and eventually built his own house instead. **Same structural pattern as [[Joan Jett]] founding Blackheart Records after every major label rejected her, or [[Taylor Swift]] re-recording her masters after losing ownership.** Different domain and scale, same move: *built the thing myself when the existing structures didn't serve.*

Function-tag: `role-authoring-at-institutional-scale`. The [[role-authoring]] disposition expressed not at the personal-professional level but at the institutional level — *I'll make the publisher, not just write for the publisher.*

Magnetic Press specializes in high-production-quality art books and translated European comics — the franco-Belgian and international tradition that sits adjacent to the [[Heavy Metal magazine]] axis already on Paul's map.
