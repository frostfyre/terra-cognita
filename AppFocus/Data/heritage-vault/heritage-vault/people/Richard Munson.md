# Richard Munson

Chandra's father. Son of [[Gene Munson]] (who built the Sedona house). Husband of [[Jenifer Munson]]. Medical professional. Gun enthusiast.

## Direct skill transmission

**Taught Paul to shoot.** Person-to-person, father-in-law to son-in-law — same direct-transmission register as [[Anita Forest]] teaching cooking, [[Glenn Vilppu|Vilppu]] teaching gesture-drawing, [[Tim Gula]] running life drawing. Different domain; same form. A real skill passed directly from one person to another in the context of a developing family relationship.

## The dance engagements

**Had Paul follow [[Chandra]] with a pistol to her professional belly dance engagements.**

This is a specific and vivid early-relationship detail. Chandra was a professional dancer performing at nightclubs, private events, restaurants — environments where a young woman performing alone could benefit from having someone present. Richard, with both protective instincts and the practical knowledge to act on them, arranged for Paul to serve that function. Armed. With a weapon Richard had taught Paul to use.

Several things operating simultaneously:

**Assessment and trust extended.** The person you send armed with your daughter to her professional engagements is someone you've made a real judgment about. Richard was evaluating Paul and simultaneously extending significant trust to him — before the marriage, before the formal commitment. That's a specific and early form of being let into the family.

**Responsibility extended early.** Paul was young — this is the high school / early reconnection period — and Richard put him in a position of real responsibility for Chandra's safety in professional contexts. That's not a casual arrangement. It shaped the early relationship between Paul and Chandra with a protective-companion dimension that predates the marriage.

**The practical-protector register.** Richard as medical professional and gun enthusiast is the same *practical competence under pressure* orientation — both medicine and marksmanship require accurate assessment, calm under stress, and the willingness to act decisively when needed. Teaching Paul to shoot and then trusting him with that responsibility is internally consistent with who Richard was.
