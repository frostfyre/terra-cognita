# Gail Carriger

Parasol Protectorate series and related works. **Steampunk Victorian England with werewolves, vampires, and the soulless** — comedy of manners as the vehicle for genuine supernatural worldbuilding.

## The specific register

**Austen-in-fantasy**: using the comedy-of-manners form (social hierarchy observation, wit, the rules of polite society as both constraint and weapon) as the vehicle for a supernatural adventure. The jokes land because the social structures are real and internally consistent; the adventure lands because the characters are fully drawn inside that social reality.

This is the **accessible-surface-with-real-stakes** structure again — Bone's register, Dr. Horrible's register, expressed in Regency-steampunk form. The comedy doesn't undercut the stakes; it earns them. Same [[commitment-to-the-bit]] structure as the rest of that cluster.

Female protagonists with intelligence and agency — Alexia Tarabotti in the Parasol Protectorate is specifically soulless (a supernatural condition) which means she can neutralize vampires and werewolves by touch, making her simultaneously an outsider to the supernatural world and the most practically powerful person in a room full of immortals. The [[outsider-as-real-perspective]] function in paranormal-romance-comedy form.
