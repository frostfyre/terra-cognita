# Metric

**The second-most-seen live act for Paul, after [[Tori Amos]].** Anchor-point musical relationship.

Canadian indie-rock band fronted by Emily Haines. Keyboard-forward, lyrically sharp, structurally tight. Embedded in the Toronto indie-rock scene that's also the broader social architecture of [[Bryan Lee O'Malley]]'s [[Scott Pilgrim]] universe — Metric is part of the same Toronto creative ecosystem the comics document.

## The closed loop with [[Scott Pilgrim]]

**Paul came to Metric *through* Scott Pilgrim.** The film's fictional band Clash at Demonhead performs "Black Sheep" — actually a Metric song with Emily Haines's vocal, even though the on-screen band is voiced by Brie Larson's character.

That makes Metric the **demonstrated case of [[music-as-worldbuilding-substrate]] working in real life**. The convention Paul appreciates in O'Malley's and [[Charles De Lint]]'s craft — using specific named music as load-bearing diegetic texture, trusting the reader to either know it or go find it — is the convention that operated on Paul to give him this anchor band.

Most absorbed-craft conventions stay theoretical. This one literally did to him what its authors describe it doing.

## Pattern-pairing with [[Tori Amos]]

Tori (American, classically-trained, piano-and-voice, mythological-confessional) and Metric (Canadian-indie, keyboard-rock, sharp-feminist, scene-embedded) are different surfaces but share important features:

- Female-fronted authorial projects with sustained creative control
- Lyrics as load-bearing, not decorative
- Musical seriousness underneath accessibility
- Bodies of work treated as ongoing authorial projects, not series of singles
