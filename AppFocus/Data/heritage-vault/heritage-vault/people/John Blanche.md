# John Blanche

**Lineage node** — the artistic director and house-style architect of Games Workshop's Citadel Miniatures / Warhammer visual culture. [[Mark Gibbons]]'s formation runs through this tradition, and Gibbons brought it into Paul's Blizzard years.

Blanche's aesthetic: gothic-baroque-grotesque, heavily medieval-Catholic-influenced, densely layered with symbolic detail, technically painterly but stylistically raw and expressionist. Very different from the Brandywine / Frazetta / American-heroic-fantasy tradition — where that tradition is dynamic, idealized, and compositionally clean, the Blanche tradition is claustrophobic, corrupted, and deliberately ugly in service of a specific dark grandeur. Both are serious traditions descended partly from the same pre-Raphaelite European roots, but they've diverged into very different aesthetic claims.

## Why this node exists

Mark Gibbons came to Blizzard from the Citadel / Games Workshop world. His presence there meant Paul was working alongside someone deeply formed in this tradition during seven years of WoW production. The Blanche aesthetic running through Gibbons is a *counterweight tradition* to the Brandywine-descended American fantasy painting Paul was also absorbing via the academy instructors and the Spectrum trips. **Both lineages were present simultaneously in the Blizzard environment**, producing productive tension rather than uniform influence.

The fact that Citadel/Blanche aesthetics and Blizzard's Sam Didier-derived house style share DNA (both descended from Frazetta, both committed to readable silhouette) while also pulling in different directions is itself worth marking — the Blizzard house style was a synthesis zone, and Mark Gibbons was one of the points of entry for the Citadel tradition's influence on that synthesis.
