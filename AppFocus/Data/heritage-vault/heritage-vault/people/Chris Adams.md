# Chris Adams

**The first technical artist Paul cites as a direct influence.** Met at [[Amazon Game Studios]]. Still contacts; relationship is *sustained but low-bandwidth* — Chris doesn't do much socializing, and the connection persists through that register rather than through active friendship.

## What Chris taught

Specific named contributions: **Python, systems composition, certain design patterns.** That list matters. Most tech-artist-to-tech-artist influence at the senior level is about pipeline-and-tooling habits or art-direction-translation-into-systems. Chris was teaching in a different register: **software-engineering disciplines applied to tech-art domains.** Systems composition and design patterns aren't typical tech-art vocabulary; they're *software architecture* vocabulary. He was teaching Paul to think about his work in software-engineering terms at a level of abstraction most tech artists don't operate at.
