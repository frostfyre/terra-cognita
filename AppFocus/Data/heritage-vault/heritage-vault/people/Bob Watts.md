# Bob Watts

**Direct instructor.** Classes and painting sessions with Paul during the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] years, via the [[Blizzard library and academy]].

**Founder of the [[Watts Atelier]] in San Diego** — one of the most important contemporary American ateliers, now run by his son Jeff Watts. The atelier teaches the **Reilly method** — Frank Reilly's structural approach to head and figure construction, descended from George Bridgman by way of the Art Students League — with an unusually rigorous and methodical curriculum. The Watts Atelier has trained an enormous number of working illustrators and concept artists across the last two decades.

Bob Watts coming to teach at Blizzard means Paul got direct exposure to **the founder of *the* California atelier**, not just a working painter who happened to teach. That's a meaningful distinction — atelier heads carry curricula, not just personal practice.

**Methodological-purist sub-cluster on the visual axis.** Together with [[Steve Huston]] (Bridgman lineage), [[Donato Giancola]] (Brandywine / Boston atelier descent), and [[James Gurney]] (observational imaginative realism), Watts is part of a coherent thread running through Paul's painting heritage: *rigorous structural observation applied to imaginative work*. This is the painting-side expression of [[methodological-purist-anchor]] — same disposition Paul holds in simulation, expressed in pedagogical lineage on the visual side. **Three named atelier-tradition figures plus Gurney is enough to be a sub-cluster rather than a coincidence.**

**Pedagogical-context note**: that Watts (an atelier head running a school) was teaching at Blizzard tells us something about how the [[Blizzard library and academy]] was curated — they weren't just hiring working painters who happened to be available, they were bringing in *people who run pedagogical institutions*. Whoever ran academy programming in Paul's era had taste and was building a real curriculum.
