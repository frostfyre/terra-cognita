# The Pinis (Wendy & Richard)

Creators of *Elfquest*. Bridge-era anchor for visual-narrative fusion, tribal/found-family mythos, long-arc worldbuilding in comics form.

**Convergent formation with [[Chandra]]** — Chandra was an Elfquest fan independently, in isolation. Paul didn't find out until well after marriage. Two people separately drawn to the same foundational aesthetic influence, who discovered the convergence years into their life together. Function-tag: `convergent-formation-discovered-retroactively`. The [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen]] fae/fantasy register traces back to Elfquest independently in both of them — not as a shared starting point, but as a parallel root that the shared venture expresses.

Critical taste-self-knowledge: **Paul fell off Elfquest when Wendy stopped drawing it.** This is a function-level signal. Loyalty was never to the Elfquest *property* — it was to **Wendy's hand specifically**. Paul reads serial comics as a *visual-author* medium, the line itself carries the meaning, and the disengagement triggered when the hand changed even though the world continued.

That instinct connects forward to the [[Heavy Metal magazine|Heavy Metal]] / [[Moebius]] / European-painter axis. Paul is not a worldbuilding-completionist reader; he's a hand-and-line reader. Apply this function tag (`visual-author-loyalty`) retroactively across the visual-arts axis.

The Pinis structurally rhyme with [[Charles De Lint]] (contemporary fae) and with [[Claremont X-Men]] (long-arc serialized worldbuilding with found family of misfits — the two most committed long-arc projects in the comics Paul absorbed).
