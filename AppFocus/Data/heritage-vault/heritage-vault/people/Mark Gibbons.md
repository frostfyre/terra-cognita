# Mark Gibbons

Colleague at [[Blizzard Entertainment]] during the WoW years; kept-in-touch since. Comes from the **Games Workshop / Citadel art tradition** — the Warhammer / 40K visual culture, [[John Blanche]]'s whole gothic-baroque-grotesque school — and brought that sensibility into WoW.

If Paul worked with Gibbons directly during the seven Blizzard years, that's a **second major art-direction lineage** running through the formation alongside [[Sam Didier]]'s. Citadel/Blanche aesthetics and Blizzard aesthetics share DNA (both descended partly from [[Frazetta]], both committed to readable silhouette and exaggerated proportion) but pull in different directions — Citadel is darker, more ornate, more grotesque, more medieval-Catholic-fever-dream. That tension is productive.

Pairs with [[Alex Horley]] as Paul's two living relationships in the working fantasy painting world.
