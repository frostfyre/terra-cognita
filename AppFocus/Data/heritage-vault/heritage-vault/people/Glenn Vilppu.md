# Glenn Vilppu

**Direct instructor** — Paul and [[John Scharmen]] attended Vilppu sessions together **during the [[Sony San Diego]] years, and after** (continuing on his independent workshop circuit post-Sony). The shared-attendance spans both the Sony chapter and the post-Sony period, making it one of the more durable shared formation threads in the Scharmen relationship — not a single shared experience but a recurring one across years.

**Placement correction from earlier draft**: Vilppu is *not* a [[Blizzard library and academy]] instructor. He's an earlier and separate layer — the Sony-era and post-Sony figure-drawing formation that predates the Blizzard chapter by over a decade. The Blizzard academy instructor roster (Gula, Huston, Gurney, Fowkes, Watts) is a distinct layer built on top of this foundation.

One of the most respected living teachers of gesture drawing and figure construction in the American animation tradition. Teaching lineage runs through Disney Feature Animation (decades of teaching there), DreamWorks, Warner Bros., and the CalArts-adjacent world. The *Vilppu Drawing Manual* and his video course series have been foundational for an enormous number of animation-trained artists and tech artists.

## The methodology

**Gesture-first, construction-second.** The living quality of a drawing comes from understanding the gesture before the anatomy; anatomy serves gesture rather than the other way around. Vilppu's specific and named contribution — a position in the long debate about how to teach figure drawing, against the anatomy-first approach.

This is a foundational layer that the Blizzard academy instruction built on top of. Gula's life-drawing sessions, Huston's Bridgman figure-construction, Watts's Reilly method — all of these were absorbed on a base that already had Vilppu's gesture-and-construction framework in it. **Formation isn't linear; Vilppu gave Paul and Scharmen a foundation that later instructors extended.**

## Shared formation with [[John Scharmen]]

Attended together — both Sony era and after. Part of the *overlapping formation layers* that give the Scharmen relationship its working-vocabulary depth. Alongside [[Chuck Jones]] (also Sony era, different axis), these two shared formations gave Paul and Scharmen a shared sub-vocabulary across both temporal and gestural aspects of animation craft.

## Lineage

Vilppu's own formation runs through the **Disney Golden Age animation tradition** and through the **figure-drawing masters of the mid-20th century** (Andrew Loomis, Bridgman, the Art Students League tradition). The gesture-first methodology is partly a reaction against overly-anatomical approaches and partly an inheritance of the animator's instinct that movement and character are primary.
