# Joan Jett

**Earliest entry on Paul's [[female-fronted-authorial-project]] cluster.** Self-choosing-childhood-tier formation, alongside [[Heart]] and [[Pat Benatar]] as the *bedrock trio* that established the cluster's criterion in Paul's taste before bridge-era figures like [[Tori Amos]] came in.

## The career as evidence of disposition

Joan Jett's career is one of the longest sustained authorial-control stories in rock:

- The Runaways (1975–1979) — punk roots, learning the music industry the hard way
- After being **rejected by every major label** for her solo work, founded **Blackheart Records in 1980** — *artist-owned label, one of the first in rock by a woman*. Released *I Love Rock 'n Roll* on it in 1981, sold five million copies.
- Continuous authorial control across forty-five years and counting

That's [[role-authoring]] **at music-business scale, predating anything else on Paul's map.** When the existing categories didn't serve the work, she authored a new one (her own label) and ran it. **Same structural pattern as Paul importing "technical artist" from film at [[3DO]] in 1998, but Jett did it in 1980 at a much harder scale.** [[Taylor Swift]]'s masters re-recording project is a contemporary version of the same move; Joan Jett is the *earliest* version of it on the map.

## Function as public-exemplar

Joan Jett also fits [[public-exemplar-of-stances-held-privately]] — *visible, sustained, unflinching enactment of artistic-control-as-non-negotiable* across decades. The refusal-edge of [[fully-present-participant]] in pop-music register, established forty-five years before the pattern got named on this map. The leather-jacket-and-electric-guitar costume is the surface; the substance is *artist-ownership-as-baseline-non-negotiable*.

## Position in the bedrock trio

[[Joan Jett]] (punk-attitude-and-stance, business-model-radicalism) + [[Heart]] (musicianship-rigor anchor) + [[Pat Benatar]] (artist-as-author-not-product in arena-rock register) are the three figures who together established the [[female-fronted-authorial-project]] cluster in Paul's taste before bridge-era figures arrived. **The cluster has a forty-five-year continuous trajectory in Paul's listening, not a contemporary-discovery shape.**
