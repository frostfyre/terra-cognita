## The product genesis story

The full chain:

1. Paul and Maxime collaborate with Michael on the 4D capture volume project
2. **The work inspires the concept for a portable volumetric studio**
3. Michael hoped Maxime might help underwrite the technology
4. Maxime didn't (Maxime's spending patterns made it unavailable)
5. **Apple introduced genlock to the iPhone Pro line** — a sudden enabling technology moment
6. Michael recognized the moment was *too hot to pass up* and built the studio independently
7. The portable volumetric capture studio shipped and **won awards in 2025**

**The genlock detail is the pivot.** Genlock is the synchronization signal allowing multiple cameras to precisely align their frame capture. Without genlock on consumer hardware, a multi-iPhone volumetric capture rig has frame-sync degradation that compromises 3D reconstruction quality. Apple adding genlock to the iPhone Pro line made a portable, consumer-hardware-based volumetric studio suddenly viable at a price point that previously required tens of thousands of dollars of professional equipment.

Michael had the technical background to recognize what that capability shift meant, the market relationships to know the demand existed, and the product-development expertise to move before anyone else did. **Selective opportunism working exactly as described.**
