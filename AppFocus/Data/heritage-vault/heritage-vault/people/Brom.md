# Brom

Met many times during the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] years. *"Met many times"* puts him in the limited-but-repeated zone — not quite mentor, but more than encounter.

One of the few living American fantasy painters working in the direct Frazetta–Wrightson lineage with a fully developed personal voice — gothic, theatrical, dark-romantic, illustrative-but-painterly. His Krampus and Devil's Rose work, and the way he runs his career as an independent painter-author, is probably relevant if Frostfyre's painted/illustrative side draws on that register at all.

Function: visible model of *the independent painter-author career* — runs his own IP, paints his own books, controls the work. A precedent worth holding for Paul's own [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen]] thinking and possibly for the post-SM positioning question.
