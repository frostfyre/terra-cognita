# Heart (Ann & Nancy Wilson)

Self-choosing-childhood-tier formation. Part of the *bedrock trio* of the [[female-fronted-authorial-project]] cluster alongside [[Joan Jett]] and [[Pat Benatar]] — the figures who established the cluster's criterion in Paul's taste before bridge-era figures arrived.

## The musicianship-rigor anchor

Where [[Joan Jett]] is the *attitude-and-business-model-radicalism* anchor of the bedrock trio, **Heart is the musicianship-rigor anchor**. Ann Wilson's voice is one of the most technically formidable in rock; Nancy Wilson is one of the great rock-rhythm-guitarists. Sisters in absolute creative control of their work since the early 70s. Survived the music-industry sexism of that era while shipping *Dreamboat Annie* (1976), *Little Queen* (1977), *Dog and Butterfly* (1978), and continuing to produce serious work for fifty-plus years.

The function-tag *musicianship-rigor anchor* matters because it's the through-line that connects Heart forward in Paul's listening to [[Tori Amos]] (conservatory-trained piano-and-voice) and to [[Björk]]'s formal-experimentalism. **The "uncompromising-authorial-vision-with-musical-seriousness" criterion of the cluster has its earliest expression in Heart's musicianship**, before Paul had vocabulary to name what he was responding to.

## Position in the bedrock trio

[[Joan Jett]] + [[Heart]] + [[Pat Benatar]] are the three figures who together established the [[female-fronted-authorial-project]] cluster in Paul's taste before bridge-era figures arrived. The cluster has a forty-five-year continuous trajectory in Paul's listening, not a contemporary-discovery shape.
