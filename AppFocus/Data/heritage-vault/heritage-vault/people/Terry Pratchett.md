# Terry Pratchett

A long-standing influence noted in the working memory layer. Sits in the same broad family as [[Robert Asprin]] and [[Phil Foglio]] — comic register held seriously, fantasy where the humor *is* the lens rather than pasted on. Discworld's underlying cosmology is fundamentally about *balance and the absurdity of absolute positions*, which is why the Pratchett affinity rhymes with the [[Michael Moorcock]] Law/Chaos/Balance instinct and with the comparative-religion-trained pluralism.

Pratchett shares with [[Mark Twain]] the satirist's voice-of-a-real-person register — both are moral writers operating inside popular forms, both trust the reader, both refuse to perform their seriousness.
