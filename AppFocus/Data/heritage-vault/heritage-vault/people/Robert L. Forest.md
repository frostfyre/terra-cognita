# Robert L. Forest

Paul's father. Son of [[JG Forest]] and [[Anita Forest]]. Met [[Chongchit Forest]] in college. Served in the Navy.

Navy service before or during college — the institutional discipline, structure, and probable international experience of military life. Then college, where he met Chongchit. The choice to marry across cultures in that era was its own kind of independence from the family pattern — JG built a commercial institution; Robert went to sea, then chose a Thai woman as his partner.

Together Robert and Chongchit created the household that produced the [[comparative-pluralist-method]] Paul carries — not as an intellectual project but as the lived reality of who his parents were. American Catholic family heritage on one side; Thai Buddhist tradition on the other. Both present daily, neither absolute.

Passed.
