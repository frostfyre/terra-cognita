# Paul Bonner

Met at [[Spectrum Fantastic Art]], sharing a booth with [[JB Monge]].

Anglo-Danish painter who did decades of Citadel / Warhammer / RuneQuest covers. One of the most beloved working-painter careers in the field. **Connects directly to the [[Mark Gibbons]] / [[John Blanche]] Citadel thread** — same broad lineage. Encountering Bonner at Spectrum closes a small loop between the Citadel-flavored influence Gibbons brought into the Blizzard chapter and the working source of that aesthetic in person.
