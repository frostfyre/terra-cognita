# Frank Herbert

Bridge-era heavyweight set member. Function: *deep-systems-ecology-and-religion*. ***Dune*** especially is an enormous achievement in worldbuilding through interlocking systems — ecology drives religion drives economics drives politics drives military doctrine drives genetic-breeding-program drives prophecy. Nothing in *Dune* is decorative; every element of the world is load-bearing for at least two other elements.

The trade-craft lesson: **a world becomes real when its systems interact, not when they're enumerated.** Worldbuilding-as-systems-design rather than worldbuilding-as-history ([[J.R.R. Tolkien]]) or worldbuilding-as-engineering-projection ([[Robert Heinlein]]).

**Direct structural debt to Paul's current work.** Procedural pipelines for sim2real are exactly *interacting systems whose realism emerges from their coupling*. Object generators feeding scene composition feeding domain randomization feeding render backend isn't an enumerated world; it's a *Dune-shaped world*, where the elements have to interact correctly for the whole to be valid.
