# Bobby Chiu

Founder of [[Lightbox Expo]] and founder / headmaster of [[Schoolism]]. Working artist with Disney/concept-art credits and a sustained educational presence around the *imaginative-illustration-as-teachable-craft* register.

## Why Bobby Chiu belongs on the heritage map

Not primarily as a direct stylistic influence but as the **architect of two institutions that bridge many figures already on this map.** Paul: *"many folks on this map connect through there."*

The structural payoff of adding Chiu isn't just a new figure; it's that [[Lightbox Expo]] and [[Schoolism]] become real nodes connecting existing nodes explicitly. Many of the named painters and educators in Paul's heritage have taught at Schoolism, attended or presented at Lightbox, or both — without these institutions as nodes, those connections are implicit rather than map-visible.

## Schoolism

Chiu founded Schoolism as one of the first serious online schools for contemporary visual development. **[[Nathan Fowkes]] teaches at Schoolism**, and many of the other named figures in Paul's painter-educator roster have presented or instructed there. It's the online education arm of the contemporary-illustration community.

## Lightbox Expo

Chiu founded Lightbox Expo as a working-illustrator gathering — different in DNA from SDCC (pop-culture-mass-market) and [[SIGGRAPH]] (technical-graphics). Closer in spirit to the old [[Spectrum Fantastic Art]] that the [[Spectrum Fantastic road trip]] visited, but with a stronger education and community-building focus.

**[[Chris Sanders]] is a recurring attendee.** [[Claire Wendling]] has taught and attended. [[Nathan Fowkes]] is regularly present. The expo functions as a bridge node across much of Paul's contemporary-illustration network.

## The institution-builder function

Chiu is the first figure on the map whose primary function is *creating the room that others are in together*. Different from [[Jeremy Cranford]]'s curator function (selecting what's in the room) — Chiu *built the institution that makes the room possible*.

That's worth marking as a distinct function-tag: **`institution-builder-who-creates-the-room`**. Might apply retroactively to a handful of others once we look for it.
