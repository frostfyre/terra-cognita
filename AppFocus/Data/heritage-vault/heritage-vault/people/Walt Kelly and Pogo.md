# Walt Kelly and Pogo

Walt Kelly (1913–1973) created *Pogo* for the New York Star in 1948, running it until his death. One of the great American newspaper strips — politically satirical, formally inventive, linguistically extraordinary, populated by the found-family community of the Okefenokee Swamp.

**Upstream of [[Jeff Smith]]** (explicitly cited as a primary influence) and therefore upstream of the *Bone* → [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen|Jackalope Junction]] lineage.

## What Pogo contributes to the lineage

**Political satire in popular cartooning form** — Kelly belongs in the same tradition as [[Mark Twain]] (satirical conscience operating through popular form) and [[Terry Pratchett]] (moral philosophy disguised as comedy), expressed in the newspaper-strip medium. The famous *"We have met the enemy and he is us"* — originally created for the first Earth Day, 1970 — is one of the great one-line summaries of collective self-defeating behavior in American culture.

**The Okefenokee found-family** — Pogo Possum, Albert the Alligator, Howland Owl, Churchy La Femme, Beauregard Bugleboy and the rest of the swamp community are another instance of the **found-family-of-misfits** structural pattern that recurs across Paul's entire literary formation: [[Robert Asprin]]'s Myth Adventures, [[The Pinis|Elfquest]]'s Wolfriders, [[Chris Claremont|Claremont's X-Men]], [[Jeff Smith|Bone]]. The swamp community is earlier than all of them and arguably the template for the American version of the pattern.

**Linguistic play and vernacular voice** — Kelly's dialect writing and wordplay sit in the same American-vernacular tradition as [[Mark Twain]]'s prose. Kelly was explicitly working in Twain's satirical register, applied to mid-20th century Cold War America. The connection isn't incidental.

**Formation through Disney** — Kelly worked at Disney before creating Pogo, absorbing the animation tradition (and thus the [[Winsor McCay]] influence that runs through Disney) before moving to newspaper comics. So Kelly is both downstream of McCay and part of the same cartooning lineage that runs forward to Smith.

## The lineage structure

[[Winsor McCay]] → Disney animation culture → Kelly → *Pogo* → [[Jeff Smith]] → *Bone* → [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen|Jackalope Junction]]

That's a five-hop lineage from McCay's formal innovations to Paul's own created work.
