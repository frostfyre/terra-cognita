# Jeremy Cranford

**One of the most important people on the heritage map.** Multi-decade colleague and friend; the human cause of the institutional pattern I previously described as "whoever curated academy programming had taste."

## Function 0: Pre-Blizzard — Art Director at [[Wizards of the Coast]]

**Before Blizzard, Jeremy was an Art Director at Wizards of the Coast** — including on [[Magic The Gathering|Magic: The Gathering]], one of the most influential AD chairs in modern fantasy illustration. The Magic AD has been a field-shaping post for decades: it decides who gets commissioned, what aesthetic registers get amplified, and how the genre's visual vocabulary evolves. Magic has trained or sustained the careers of a substantial percentage of the working contemporary fantasy painters.

This is the recontextualizing fact: **Jeremy didn't develop his curatorial taste at Blizzard — he brought it from WotC**, where he'd already been actively shaping the contemporary fantasy painting field through commission decisions for years. The atelier-heads-and-Bridgman-masters roster at the [[Blizzard library and academy]] isn't the work of a games-industry AD who happened to know about painters; it's the work of *a former Magic: The Gathering Art Director*, exercising an already-developed curatorial vocabulary in a new institutional context.

**The biographical rhyme**: Paul learned and became obsessed with Magic during his [[Sony San Diego]] years (early-mid 90s — Magic launched 1993). Paul did not know, at the time of meeting Jeremy at Blizzard, that Jeremy had been the AD of Magic during that formative period. **The art Paul was studying through gameplay obsession in 1995 at Sony was being commissioned by the man Paul would later co-curate an art education program with at Blizzard.** That's a real loop, only visible because this heritage map got built carefully.

Function-tag: `retroactively-discovered-upstream-influence`. Different from instructor, different from colleague, different from passively-absorbed influence. The figure was shaping Paul's visual education *before the relationship existed*. There's likely a sub-graph of *painters Jeremy commissioned for Magic that Paul absorbed before knowing Jeremy existed* — the map could excavate that thread.

## Function 1: Curator of the [[Blizzard library and academy]]

Jeremy ran the academy programming. Every good thing about how that roster was structurally coherent — bringing in atelier heads ([[Bob Watts]]), Bridgman-tradition figure masters ([[Steve Huston]]), both major color-and-light lineages ([[James Gurney]], [[Nathan Fowkes]]), foundations through advanced — wasn't accident. **That was Jeremy's taste.**

Every painting-side instructor node in this map has Jeremy as the upstream cause. He's the *author* of the institutional context that gave Paul that whole roster, the workshops, the visiting-artist sessions, the library curation.

**Paul was a regular participant in the academy programs Jeremy ran**: attended workshops regularly, gave feedback, occasionally helped with cleanup and other practical aspects of running the sessions. *Not* co-curator — that's overselling. Jeremy was the curator; Paul was a deeply engaged participant who treated the program as if it were his to extract maximum value from. Function-tag: `fully-present-participant` — show up to the thing, all the way. That's a real disposition, distinct from curatorship and worth marking on its own terms.
