# Fiona Apple

Paul's framing: *"fun."* That's an interesting register choice — most listeners describe her work as devastating, intense, or technically formidable. *Fetch the Bolt Cutters* (2020) was a critical event widely received as one of the rawest and craft-rigorous albums in recent memory.

Two readings of "fun":

1. *Enjoyable to listen to despite or alongside the intensity* — the technical precision and rhythmic invention as their own pleasure
2. *Engaged with more lightly than the [[Tori Amos]] / [[Björk]] anchor relationships* — present in the listening but not at anchor weight

Either way, places her tonally at a lower-weight tier than the anchors. Sits in the [[female-fronted-authorial-project]] cluster formally but isn't a sustained-relationship entry like Tori or Björk.
