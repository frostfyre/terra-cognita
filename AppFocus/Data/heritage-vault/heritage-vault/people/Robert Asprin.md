# Robert Asprin

Bridge-era anchor on the comic-fantasy register. Center of gravity: ***Myth Adventures*** — punning, sitcom-structured, dimension-hopping, found-family-of-misfits. Specifically the *American* comic-fantasy tradition, where [[Terry Pratchett]] is the British one. Together they give Paul a deep bench in this lineage, with [[Phil Foglio]] as the comics-side cousin.

Also created **Thieves' World** — the shared-world anthology series, multiple authors writing in a single carefully-constructed setting. Foundational shared-world experiment in fantasy publishing. Direct relative of MMO worldbuilding, of franchise IP work, of the bible-driven content production Paul would later do at [[Blizzard Entertainment]].

The found-family-of-misfits structural pattern in Myth (Skeeve, Aahz, Tananda, Chumley, Gleep) recurs across many of Paul's loved works: Elfquest's Wolfriders, [[Claremont X-Men]], the Bronze Age comics teams. Load-bearing structural pattern.
