# Nathan Fowkes

**Direct instructor.** Classes and painting sessions with Paul during the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] years, via the [[Blizzard library and academy]]. Paul still follows his published work.

Long career at DreamWorks Animation. One of the most respected working teachers of *color script, value structure, and atmospheric painting* in contemporary visual development. Online courses ([[Schoolism]]), books (*Color Beyond the Wheel*), and ongoing teaching practice have shaped a huge swath of contemporary visual development artists.

**Same broad function as [[James Gurney]], but from the *animation production* side rather than the *imaginative realist* side.** Together they give Paul exposure to *both* major lineages of contemporary color-and-light teaching.

The Schoolism / Fowkes lineage tends to emphasize **design-as-color** and **mood-as-structure** — the production side where every image has a job in a sequence. Useful counterpart to Gurney's observational-imaginative-realist posture.
