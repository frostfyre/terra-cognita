# Maxime C. Peroumal

Long-term professional contact in the VR and spatial computing space. Currently running **Impossible**, a stealth-mode startup in the VR space. Previously: Radiant Images collaboration with Paul on 4D capture volume work.

## The early formation — autodidact frontier technologist

**Built HMD technology in his mid-teens.** Wrote open-source drivers for VR headsets. This is the autodidact-foundational-stream disposition in technical form — same register as Paul studying [[Renderman]] via [[Blue Moon Render Tools]] before there was a professional context for it. Maxime was at the frontier of spatial computing before the frontier had a name.

The open-source drivers specifically place him in the early VR community — probably the Oculus DK1/DK2 era (2012–2014), when the indie VR developer community was writing their own tooling because official SDKs were immature. **Building the thing rather than waiting for it to exist.** Same family as [[Joan Jett]] founding Blackheart Records, [[Jeff Smith]] self-publishing Bone — different domain, same disposition.

This early formation matters for understanding who Maxime is as a collaborator: he's a visionary who thinks in capabilities rather than constraints, which is both why he's exciting to work with and why the working relationship requires active management.

## The Radiant Images collaboration

Paul and Maxime worked with [[Michael Mansouri]] at [[Radiant Images]] on a **4D capture volume project**. Paul's framing: *"physical data capture pipelines to the maximum possible given budgets."*

That framing is precise — this was **delivery under constraint**, not theoretical pipeline design. Maximum fidelity given a real budget ceiling, with the ceiling being real. Same seam Paul navigates in the [[[[Sentient Machines]] pipeline|Sentient Machines synthetic data work]], expressed in physical capture rather than procedural generation.

Out of this work arose **Radiant Images' new portable capture studio** — a shipped commercial product. Paul and Maxime were part of its development lineage. **Direct contribution to a product that exists in the world** — a different kind of heritage entry than most on the map.

## The calibrated trust relationship

**Maxime has an unfortunate tendency to underestimate the costs of what he asks for.**

That's a precise professional characterization of a real pattern. Paul knows this pattern, works with Maxime anyway, and factors it in. That's *calibrated trust* rather than unconditional trust — sophisticated relational management.

The cost-underestimation pattern is probably downstream of the visionary-who-thinks-in-capabilities formation. Someone who built HMD technology as a teenager, who thinks about what's technically possible before thinking about what it costs, will consistently underestimate production costs. It's the same capacity that makes him a frontier technologist and the same capacity that creates the gap between vision and delivery reality.

## The persona/mystique dimension

Maxime maintains a "mystique/ego" — a constructed professional persona befitting the autodidact-frontier-technologist-founder identity. The spending that sometimes creates problems is partly in service of maintaining that persona. **Paul has seen him with shields down on occasion** — which means Paul has a relationship that goes past the persona to the actual person. That's its own form of trust and intimacy, distinct from the professional calibration.

Worth marking as a pattern: founders and frontier technologists often construct and invest in a persona as a professional tool. The persona attracts investment, talent, partnerships. The spending that maintains it is a real cost. Whether it's sustainable depends on the underlying fundamentals — which is the open question with Maxime and Impossible.

## The Radiant underwriting moment

When Michael was developing the portable volumetric studio concept (seeded by Paul and Maxime's 4D capture collaboration), **Michael hoped Maxime might help underwrite the technology.** Maxime didn't — the spending pattern and persona costs probably made it unavailable. Michael recognized the moment was too hot to pass up and built it independently. The product that emerged won awards this year.

Paul's collaboration seeded the concept; Maxime's potential underwriting didn't materialize; Michael built it anyway. **Paul's contribution to the Radiant product is real but indirect** — conceptual substrate and early collaboration rather than development or funding.

UE5.x and Metahuman-based virtual volumetric capture — the most recent Impossible collaboration before the Sentient Machines engagement. Bridging physical human capture and synthetic Metahuman parametric representation. **The physical/synthetic seam in direct form** — the same seam Paul navigates at Sentient Machines, expressed through Epic's tooling and Maxime's VR/capture expertise.

This work ended fairly abruptly just prior to gaining Harry/Sentient Machines as a client. The pipeline-and-capture expertise was probably part of what made Paul legible to Harry as a hire.
