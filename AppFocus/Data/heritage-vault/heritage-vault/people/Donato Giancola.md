# Donato Giancola

Met at [[Spectrum Fantastic Art]] in Kansas City — **breakfast.** That's a real conversation, not a signing line. Goes in tier-two ("limited but meaningful") with weight.

One of the most technically rigorous living fantasy painters, deeply schooled in the Western academic tradition (Pyle, Wyeth, the Brandywine school by way of Boston atelier training), and a serious teacher of his craft.

Function for Paul: exemplar of *technical rigor as the foundation of imaginative work* — a methodological-purist position in painting that rhymes with Paul's [[methodological-purist-anchor]] disposition in simulation and synthetic data.
