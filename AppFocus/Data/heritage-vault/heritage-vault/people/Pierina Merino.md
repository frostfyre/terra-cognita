When the chapter ends, their attention is fully forward. Paul reads this clearly and accurately rather than treating the distance as personal.

**Tier: understood-distance** — distinct from the low-bandwidth-sustained-contact tier ([[Chris Adams]], [[Rudy Cortez]], [[Matthew Bell]]) where there's ongoing mutual presence even without much active contact.
