Very common in technically-excellent young founders; a developmental stage rather than a fixed character trait. **Energetic.** Worth marking as a texture of the working relationship — energy in a founder is contagious and productive when the technical problems are genuinely hard and genuinely interesting.

## The working relationship

**"He seems to trust me completely, but isn't shy about correcting me when I'm wandering into the weeds."**

This combination is harder to achieve than either component alone and is the most telling characterization in Paul's description. Complete trust alongside confident correction requires:

- Clear epistemic self-confidence about his own technical territory (he knows what he knows)
- Genuine respect for Paul's domain expertise (he trusts Paul's judgment where Paul is expert)
- Absence of defensive ego in the correction (he corrects without making it political)

**This is structurally the opposite of the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] chapter's absentee boss** — who neither trusted Paul nor had the expertise to correct him productively. Harry's combination (full trust *and* competent correction) is the working relationship Paul has been selecting for across the whole [[high-trust-relationship-architecture]] arc.

The confident correction is also a mark of someone who can tell when Paul is outside his domain — which means Harry's technical competence in robotics is genuine and specific enough that he can detect the boundaries. That's exactly the [[assume-I-know-nothing]] register Paul wants to be in: someone who will tell him when he's wrong.

**"I very much enjoy working with him."** Four words that carry real weight on this map. Paul respects and trusts many people in the sustained-contact tier; *genuine enjoyment* is a different register. Worth marking explicitly.
