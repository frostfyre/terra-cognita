# Frank Reilly method

**Lineage node** — the specific pedagogical system taught at the [[Watts Atelier]] (founded by [[Bob Watts]], now run by Jeff Watts) and the basis of that institution's curriculum.

Frank Reilly (1906–1967) was an Art Students League instructor who developed a systematic approach to head and figure construction derived from **George Bridgman**'s structural method but organized into a more explicit step-by-step system. Reilly's method emphasizes: specific planes of the head, a defined approach to the shadow pattern, structural landmarks that can be learned sequentially. It's the most *teachable* of the major figure-drawing traditions — systematized enough that students can follow a clear progression, rigorous enough that the results hold up structurally.

The lineage: **Bridgman → Reilly → Watts Atelier** is one of the cleaner pedagogical transmission lines in contemporary American atelier training.

## Why this node exists

Bob Watts brought the Reilly method to the [[Blizzard library and academy]] workshops. When Paul attended those sessions, he was absorbing a specific and named pedagogical tradition — not just "figure drawing" generically but *Reilly-method figure drawing* with a specific claim about how to see and construct the head and figure. **That tradition has a name and a lineage and should be visible on the map.**
