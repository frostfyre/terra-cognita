# Public exemplar of stances held privately

A function-tag that surfaced through the [[Taylor Swift]] entry. Different from aesthetic-anchor relationships and different from formative-influence relationships.

## Definition

**Public figures whose visibly-articulated values, business decisions, or creative postures externalize patterns Paul holds as private dispositions.** Paul respects them not primarily for their craft (though the craft may also be respected), but for their *public enactment* of stances Paul has independently arrived at and operates by.

The function isn't influence in the usual direction (figure shapes Paul). It's more like *recognition under already-developed values* — Paul reads the public figure's stance and finds it isomorphic to something he already does, valued at culturally-visible scale.

## Confirmed entries

- **[[Taylor Swift]]** — the masters re-recording project as [[role-authoring]] at pop-cultural scale; the Spotify/Apple withdrawals as the refusal-edge of [[fully-present-participant]] in full public view; sustained-authorial-control-over-decades; *consistency of articulated position over years*. Paul: *"not quite a Swiftie but I love her work, attitude and expressed public philosophy."*
- **[[Björk]]** — Paul's correction: *her philosophy plays into my enjoyment of her music*. Articulated values around environmental stewardship, Icelandic geological-cultural belonging, gender and creative agency, and the responsibility of the artist toward the work-as-craft are part of why the music lands.
- **[[Joan Jett]]** — *visible, sustained, unflinching enactment of artistic-control-as-non-negotiable* across forty-five years. Founded Blackheart Records in 1980 — the earliest manifestation of [[role-authoring]] at music-business scale on the entire heritage map. The leather-jacket-and-electric-guitar costume is the surface; the substance is *artist-ownership-as-baseline-non-negotiable*.

## The pattern is more pervasive in music than initially surfaced

Earlier draft of this concept had only Taylor as a confirmed music-axis entry. Paul's corrections (Björk's philosophy, the depth of the Joan Jett relationship) showed that **the public-exemplar function is widely operative in his music listening, not limited to a single figure.** The [[female-fronted-authorial-project]] cluster and the public-exemplar function overlap substantially — not coincidentally, since *uncompromising authorial vision* tends to produce artists with publicly-articulated values.

Likely retroactive application: any anchor-tier music figure on Paul's map probably has at least some public-exemplar function alongside the aesthetic-anchor function. Worth checking individually rather than auto-tagging.

## Probable other entries (open)

The function probably applies elsewhere on the map and is worth surfacing in future passes:

- **[[Brom]]** — independent painter-author career, control-of-own-IP, the visible model of *running-the-art-business-on-your-own-terms*. Structurally same function as Taylor at painting scale rather than pop-music scale.
- Probably others in the contemporary fantasy painting field who've built independent careers (Donato's atelier teaching is partly this; Gurney's Dinotopia + nature-of-art content is also partly this)
- Possibly figures in the SIGGRAPH-classical-rigor world who've held their methodological line publicly while the field shifted

## Why this matters

Distinguishing this function from passive influence keeps the map honest. **Paul didn't learn his disposition from Taylor; he recognized it in her.** The function-tag captures that asymmetry.

It also has predictive power: when Paul names admiration for a public figure that doesn't easily fit either *formative-influence* or *aesthetic-anchor* category, the question to ask is *"what private stance of Paul's is this public figure enacting visibly?"* — that question usually clarifies the relationship.

## Connection to [[adult-aesthetic-landing]]

This function-tag is a common occupant of the adult-aesthetic-landing tier. Both involve *recognition under already-developed criteria* rather than *formation of criteria*. They're related but distinct: adult-aesthetic-landing covers the diagnostic of current taste; public-exemplar covers the diagnostic of current values and stances. Different lenses on the same broader phenomenon of *Paul-at-current-disposition assessing the world*.
