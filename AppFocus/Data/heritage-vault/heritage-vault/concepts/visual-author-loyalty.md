# Visual author loyalty

Paul reads serial visual media as a **visual-author** medium. The line itself carries the meaning; the hand is the work. Loyalty attaches to the *hand*, not to the *property*.

Canonical evidence: **Paul fell off Elfquest when [[The Pinis|Wendy Pini]] stopped drawing it.** The franchise continued; Paul's relationship to it ended. Loyalty was to Wendy's hand specifically, not to the world she'd built.

This connects forward to:

- **[[Moebius]]** — the deepest well in the [[Heavy Metal magazine|Heavy Metal]] cohort. The image-as-worldbuilding instinct.
- **The whole Heavy Metal / Epic / European-painter axis** — Bilal, Druillet, Corben, the painterly tradition Paul has been "all of them, but Moebius especially" toward.
- **[[Wei Wang]]** at [[Blizzard Entertainment]] — sustained proximity to a single hand that defined an institution's visual identity.
- **The [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen]] booth** — Paul's own work, where the hand is the brand.

**Paul is not a worldbuilding-completionist reader; he is a hand-and-line reader.** This applies retroactively across the whole visual axis and clarifies the function of many of the named figures.
