# High-trust relationship architecture

A meta-observation about how Paul's career has actually been structured.

**Paul's career has been structured by a small number of high-trust long-term relationships, each of whom is the human cause of an institutional pattern that looks like luck or taste from the outside.**

Four named so far in the heritage map:

- **[[John Scharmen]]** — Sony San Diego friendship that became the channel into [[Blizzard Entertainment]] (with [[Steve Aguilar]]). The Lich King-through-Legion run and everything downstream of it (the [[Blizzard library and academy]] exposure, [[Alex Horley]], [[Mark Gibbons]], the [[Spectrum Fantastic road trip]]) traces back to Scharmen as the channel.
- **[[Steve Aguilar]]** — the other half of the Sony-to-Blizzard transit; long-arc colleague spanning roughly three decades.
- **[[Jeremy Cranford]]** — the curator of the academy programming. Every painting-side instructor on the map (Gurney, Huston, Watts, Fowkes, Gula) traces back to Jeremy's taste, which itself was already field-shaping during his pre-Blizzard chair as Art Director at [[Wizards of the Coast]] including on [[Magic The Gathering]]. Also the channel from Blizzard into [[FTX Good Luck Games]] post-Amazon.
- **[[Joey Ray Hall]]** — a huge presence at Blizzard, then the person who hired Paul into [[Amazon Game Studios]]. Paul: *"I wanted to learn from him."* Adds a new function-tag, `deliberately-chosen-mentor-figure` — the first explicitly active apprenticeship-orientation on the map.

## Why this matters for the map's framing

The "standing on shoulders of giants" framing applies, but the specific shape is more like *a small number of giants, deeply known, whose presence in Paul's life caused most of the rooms he ended up in*.

The institutional patterns I kept observing in the conversation — the Sony cohort, the academy roster's coherence, the post-Amazon studio chapter pattern, the Amazon chapter itself — each had a *named human* underneath them. Paul's career didn't randomly intersect with these institutions. **His durable relationships brought him into the institutions, or brought the institutions to him.**

This is also bidirectional: Paul has been brought *into* rooms (Scharmen → Blizzard) and Paul has *followed* people into rooms (Cranford → Good Luck Games; Joey Ray Hall → Amazon). Function-tag: `network-mover-bidirectional`.

## The dispositional through-line (newly visible)

A pattern that became visible across multiple named figures: **the people Paul maintains contact with from professional chapters consistently share an epistemic register with him.**

Specifically: low BS tolerance, work-focused-rather-than-political, present-without-performance, real connection that doesn't require performance of connection. By Paul's descriptions across the conversation, this register fits **[[John Scharmen]], [[Steve Aguilar]], [[Jeremy Cranford]], [[Joey Ray Hall]], [[Chris Adams]], and [[Rudy Cortez]]** — every named long-arc figure on the heritage map.

That's not coincidence; it's selection. **Paul consistently forms durable working relationships with people in this disposition.** The trust-relationships-drive-career structural pattern (the original claim of this concept) operates *within a specific dispositional register*. Two together is more accurate than either alone:

- **Structurally**: trust relationships move Paul between rooms (career architecture)
- **Dispositionally**: those trust relationships consistently form with people who share a particular register (selection mechanism)

The structural pattern is downstream of the dispositional one. Paul doesn't form trust with random colleagues and then ride those relationships into new chapters; he selectively forms trust with a specific kind of person, and *those* relationships become career-architectural.

That's relevant for the [[Sentient Machines]] engagement: Harry is the current named founder-relationship, and the question of whether he becomes a long-arc trust-figure is partly a question of whether he sits in the same dispositional register. Worth attending to.

## Open: probably more named figures underneath this pattern

The pattern is consistent enough that there are likely other high-trust long-term relationships not yet on the map who account for other institutional patterns:

- The post-Amazon turbulent stretch ([[Flickplay]], [[Gala Games]], [[Hypixel Studios]]) probably has at least one more named figure underneath it. Maxime / Impossible is one candidate; there are likely others.
- The Amazon-era chapter beyond Joey Ray Hall — peers, leads, ADs Paul actually worked with under him on [[New World]] or in marketing.
- The current [[Sentient Machines]] engagement has Harry as the named founder; whether Harry becomes a long-arc relationship or stays chapter-specific is a live question rather than a settled one.

## The grateful-and-accurate framing

This concept exists partly to correct a default reading of the heritage map. Reading the map as *Paul absorbed influences from these many figures* misses the more interesting and accurate truth: **Paul's career has been carried by a small number of deeply trusted relationships, and the heritage map's breadth is downstream of that trust.**

It's a more grateful framing because it names the people specifically. It's a more accurate framing because it explains why the map's institutional patterns aren't random.
