# Hair bands (handful)

Paul's framing: *"a handful of hair bands."* That precise word matters — *a handful*, somewhat distanced, not "I was deep into hair metal" or "I loved hair bands."

That register places hair bands as **tonal pleasure of the era** — the music that was on, that was fun, that wasn't deeply formative but was part of the soundscape. Lower-weight tier than [[Metallica]]'s formative role in the heavy register. Hair bands aren't an aesthetic anchor; they're context.
