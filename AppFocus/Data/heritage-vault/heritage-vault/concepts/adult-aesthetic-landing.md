# Adult aesthetic landing

A tier-category the heritage map needs but didn't have until [[Scott Pilgrim]] surfaced as an entry.

## Definition

**Things absorbed under already-developed *evaluative tools*, where the recognition is *"this is something done right by my standards"* rather than *"this is shaping my standards."***

Important refinement (per Paul's [[eternal-student]] correction): adult-aesthetic-landing entries are *not* absorbed *post-formation*. Paul's formation is ongoing — he's *constantly forming* across every era. **What this tier captures is the moment when criteria are mature enough to evaluate new entries against**, even though the criteria themselves continue developing. The taste isn't *finally* built; it's always under continued construction with evaluative tools that have themselves matured.

Distinct from:

- **Pre-conscious / childhood** layer — Twain, Greek myth, Andersen, Pasteur. Absorbed before taste formed.
- **Self-choosing childhood** — DC comics, then Marvel. Absorbed as taste was forming.
- **Bridge era** — Moorcock, Heinlein, Herbert, Tolkien, the Pinis, De Lint, Le Guin. Absorbed *as* taste formed.
- **Professional formation** — the [[Blizzard library and academy]] instructors, SIGGRAPH-window technical figures. Absorbed as practice was being built.

**Adult aesthetic landing** is a distinct fifth tier: things that hit when taste was already settled, where the recognition is *"this is something done right by my standards"* rather than *"this is shaping my standards."*

## Why this tier needs its own name

The other tiers are about **formation**. This tier is about **recognition under already-developed taste**. The function in someone's life is genuinely different:

- Childhood entries shape what you can imagine
- Bridge entries shape what you can name
- Adult landings tell you something about your *current* aesthetic — they're a diagnostic of where your taste actually is, because you came to them as someone whose preferences were already formed

That makes adult-landing entries especially useful for *self-knowledge*, not just historical-influence mapping. They reveal what registers as good once you have the criteria.

## Probably more entries to surface

Many of the [[Blizzard Entertainment]]-era painters Paul met as colleagues rather than as formation could be reframed under this tier when he was already experienced. The post-bridge "settled adult reader" period of literature (still an open thread) likely produces entries here. Music beyond Tori (childhood/teen formation) and Metric (Scott Pilgrim adult-landing) probably has both formation-tier and adult-landing-tier figures that would be useful to disambiguate.

When future entries get added, **ask: did Paul absorb this *while* his taste was forming, or *as* an artifact of the taste he already had?** The answer tells you which tier it belongs in.
