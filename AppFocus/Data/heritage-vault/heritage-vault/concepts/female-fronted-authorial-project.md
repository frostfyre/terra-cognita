# Female-fronted authorial project

A confirmed cluster on Paul's music axis with **a forty-five-year continuous trajectory in his listening, not a contemporary-discovery shape.** The criterion was already operating in Paul's taste by his early teens via the bedrock trio of [[Joan Jett]], [[Heart]], and [[Pat Benatar]] — substantially earlier than the comics axis or the bridge-era literature heavyweights.

## The bedrock trio (formation tier)

- **[[Joan Jett]]** — *attitude-and-business-model-radicalism* anchor. Founded Blackheart Records in 1980 after every major label rejected her — [[role-authoring]] at music-business scale, **earliest manifestation of that pattern on the entire heritage map**.
- **[[Heart]]** (Ann & Nancy Wilson) — *musicianship-rigor* anchor. Voice-and-guitar technical formidability sustained across fifty-plus years.
- **[[Pat Benatar]]** — *classically-trained vocal rigor* anchor. Operatic background, MTV-era authorial control over image and stage, sustained creative partnership with Neil Giraldo.

These three are the *formation* of the cluster. The bridge-era and contemporary entries (Tori, Björk, Metric, Taylor, PJ, Fiona) are continuations of an axis already established by these three.

## Bridge / lifelong tier

- **[[Tori Amos]]** — longest-running live anchor across decades; conservatory-trained-piano-confessional, mythological-confessional-interior register
- **[[Björk]]** — multi-decade relationship from the Sugarcubes era onward; post-genre-formal-experimentalist, the limit case of authorial control in the cluster; **philosophy plays into Paul's enjoyment of the music** (also a [[public-exemplar-of-stances-held-privately]] entry)

## Adult-aesthetic-landing tier

- **[[Metric]]** — second-most-seen live; introduced via [[Scott Pilgrim]]; demonstrates [[music-as-worldbuilding-substrate]] working in real life
- **[[Taylor Swift]]** — *"not quite a Swiftie but I love her work, attitude and expressed public philosophy"*; also a [[public-exemplar-of-stances-held-privately]] entry; loves the music itself across all eras while declining to costume the engagement in fan-culture identity

## Pattern-fit-but-lower-weight

- **[[PJ Harvey]]** — *"pretty good"*; political-historical-confrontational register
- **[[Fiona Apple]]** — *"fun"*; acknowledged-but-not-anchor

## The refined through-line

Initial draft of this pattern proposed *"female-fronted authorial projects with sustained creative control"* as the criterion. **PJ Harvey landing at *"pretty good"* despite formally fitting that pattern told us the actual through-line is narrower.** With the bedrock trio now placed, an even sharper version of the criterion is visible:

**Mythological / interior / formal-experimentalist register, under uncompromising authorial vision, with technical-or-craft seriousness underneath the surface.**

The bedrock trio prefigures all three components: Heart for musicianship-rigor, Benatar for classical-vocal-training-as-foundation, Joan Jett for the *uncompromising authorial vision* component (and its business-model expression). The bridge and contemporary entries develop the *interior / mythological / formal-experimentalist* register on top of those foundations.

## The public-exemplar overlap is more pervasive than initially surfaced

Earlier draft had only [[Taylor Swift]] as a [[public-exemplar-of-stances-held-privately]] entry. Per Paul's correction on Björk (*her philosophy plays into my enjoyment of her music*) and the structural fit of [[Joan Jett]]'s lifelong public stance, the public-exemplar function is **more pervasive across this cluster than originally noted.**

Paul listens to artists whose articulated values he's taken seriously alongside their craft. That's a real pattern in his music-listening, not an outlier limited to Taylor. The cluster overlaps substantially with the public-exemplar function, not coincidentally — the *uncompromising-authorial-vision* criterion that defines the cluster also tends to produce artists with articulated values they hold publicly.
