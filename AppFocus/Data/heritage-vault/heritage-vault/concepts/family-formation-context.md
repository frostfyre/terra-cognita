# Family formation context

The household Paul grew up in held multiple religious and cultural traditions simultaneously — not as a deliberate comparative-religion project but as the lived reality of the family's composition.

## The multi-tradition structure

**Paternal grandparents** — Catholic. Funded Paul's Catholic education. Lived down the street; Sunday dinners as a weekly tradition. [[JG Forest]] as the grandfather figure — inventor, founder of ISF/Silo Guard, the recognition-and-translation intelligence in direct family relationship.

**Mother** — Thai Buddhist background. Already on the map as part of the [[comparative-pluralist-method]] formation. The Thai Buddhist household atmosphere running alongside the Catholic grandparents and Catholic schooling.

**The comparative-religion class** — already noted on the map as a formal education context that developed the comparative-pluralist method. The formal instruction built on a foundation that was already lived: Paul had been navigating multiple coherent religious frameworks simultaneously since childhood simply by belonging to his family.

## The Catholic education formation

Catholic schooling funded by grandparents. Specific formation contributions:
- **Liturgical rhythm** — the structured cycle of the church calendar; formation through repeated ritual
- **Systematic theology and moral philosophy** — ethics as a formal subject rather than ambient cultural transmission
- **Catholic visual culture** — the iconographic tradition, devotional art, the proportions of sacred architecture. This is real aesthetic formation: the Byzantine-through-medieval Catholic visual tradition is part of the same decorative-surface-as-sacred register as [[Sergio Toppi]]'s decorated pages and [[Cartoon Saloon]]'s illuminated-manuscript aesthetic
- **The medieval world** as a lived cultural reference — connecting to the [[Society for Creative Anachronism]] formation and the [[lion-in-winter-my-fair-lady|Lion in Winter]] annual viewing

## The Sunday dinner ritual

Weekly gathering at the grandparents' house down the street. Combined with Walt Disney Sunday nights on television, Sunday was the ritual day of Paul's childhood — the day defined by family gathering and repeated shared experience. The **ritual-formation structure** that appears across the whole map (annual viewings, repeated sought-out works, weekly conventions) starts here in childhood domestic rhythm.

## What this produces

The [[comparative-pluralist-method]] concept note says Paul holds "multiple coherent frameworks, no absolute truth." That disposition wasn't formed only in a comparative religion class — it was formed by *growing up inside a family that held multiple traditions simultaneously without resolving them*. Catholic education paid for by Catholic grandparents; Thai Buddhist mother; Sunday dinners with the same grandparents; Disney on Sunday nights. Multiple coherent frameworks, all real, none absolute.
