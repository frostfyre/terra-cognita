# Autodidact foundational stream

A self-directed training layer that **predates and underlies** the studio-curated formation chapters at [[Blizzard Entertainment]] and [[Amazon Game Studios]]. Distinct from the [[dual-training-convergence]] in three specific ways:

- **Temporally earlier** — operative from the early Sony years and the [[SIGGRAPH]] window opening in 1995, before either studio chapter
- **Epistemologically different** — *self-directed* and *speculative* rather than *studio-curated*; Paul was building expertise nobody at his job was teaching him
- **Foundational rather than parallel** — the dispositions this stream established (understand the algorithm underneath the tool; study what's not yet professionally relevant) are *prerequisites* for what the later studio formations could build on top

## The constituent elements

- **[[SIGGRAPH]]** annually 1995–early 2010s — the field's research frontier, attended continuously
- **[[Renderman]] study via [[Blue Moon Render Tools]]** — Larry Gritz's Renderman-compliant renderer, the way to actually run Renderman-spec scenes without Pixar's proprietary tools. Studied the *algorithmic foundations of rendering*, not just the user-facing surface.
- **[[PowerAnimator and the SGI era]]** — early Maya, SGI workstation hardware (Indigo, Indy, Octane, Onyx). The platform context where realtime 3D and procedural work were possible at all.
- **Specific interest registers**: *procedural rendering* and *modeling in realtime*. Both were research-frontier topics at SIGGRAPH in that window; both became professionally relevant later.

## The forward-investment-self-directed-study function

Function-tag this surfaces: **`forward-investment-self-directed-study`** — the disposition of *building expertise on speculation, before there's a professional context that demands it*.

Adjacent to but distinct from [[eternal-student]]. Where eternal-student is *engaging fully with what's there to learn*, forward-investment-self-directed-study is *seeking out what to learn before anyone is offering it*. Same broad family of disposition, different specific behavior.

Paul was studying Renderman shaders and BMRT alongside SIGGRAPH paper streams *while doing PSX-era animation work professionally* — the day job was animation; the speculative training was rendering algorithms and proceduralism. **That speculation paid off a decade later** when procedural pipelines, programmable shading, and realtime-procedural generation became central to what tech artists do.

## Why this is foundational rather than parallel

The [[methodological-purist-anchor]] disposition — *understand the mechanism underneath, don't accept the surface result alone* — has its earliest expression in the choice to **study Renderman shaders rather than just use a renderer**. That choice precedes Cranford's curated academy by a decade and the Amazon ML training by closer to two decades. **The disposition was established in this autodidact stream and the later studio chapters built on it.**

Without this stream, the [[Blizzard library and academy]] participation would have been classical-painting-pedagogy on top of a less-rigorous technical base. With it, the academy participation was *another formation layer added to a foundation that already had algorithmic-rendering depth*. Same for Amazon — [[Chris Adams]]'s software-engineering teaching, [[Olcay Boz]]'s production-ML pedagogy, and [[Ali Arsanjani]]'s enterprise-AI architecture all built on a foundation Paul had been laying since the mid-90s.

## Connection to the [[dual-training-convergence]]

The dual-training-convergence concept (Blizzard visual-craft + Amazon software-and-ML, converging in current sim2real work) is **structurally complete only when this autodidact stream is named as its prerequisite**. The honest open question in that node — *is there a third training stream the map hasn't surfaced yet?* — is now answered: **yes, and it's foundational rather than parallel**.

The current [[Sentient Machines pipeline|Sentient Machines pipeline work]] therefore draws on three streams:

1. **Foundational autodidact** — algorithmic rendering, proceduralism, realtime work; SIGGRAPH/Renderman/BMRT/SGI era; mid-90s onward
2. **Blizzard visual-craft** — classical-painting pedagogy, tech-art at MMO scale; 2008–2016
3. **Amazon software-and-ML** — software-engineering disciplines, production-ML, enterprise-AI architecture; ~2016–2022

Each adds a register the others don't have. **The current work is illegible from any single-stream vantage**, which is partly why Paul's professional positioning has consistently been peer-credentialed-rather-than-consumer-credentialed.

## What this answers about Paul's whole arc

The studio chapters didn't *make* Paul a methodological purist or a procedural-thinker. He was already those things, *because of this autodidact stream*. The studios offered additional layers; the foundation was self-laid.

That's a real upgrade to the map's accuracy and probably to Paul's self-knowledge. **The professional voice that articulates "Building the Matrix" rests on a self-directed foundation built decades before there was a market for the synthesis.**
