# Methodological purist anchor

Paul has a strong methodological-purist streak, but it's **pluralist purism**, not single-tradition purism. ([[comparative-pluralist-method|Pluralist about *frameworks*, purist about *rigor*.]])

Core demand: **examinability.** Classical simulation papers ship with derivations, stability proofs, comparisons to ground truth, identifiable failure modes. ML-pervaded approaches often ship with benchmarks, ablations, and "looks good in our test scenes." Different intellectual cultures.

For someone whose formation runs through:

- **Pasteur on the bookshelf** (the empirical-and-wondrous on the same shelf)
- **[[Theravada Buddhism]]** via mother (grounded, undramatic attention)
- **[[comparative-pluralist-method|Comparative-religion-trained skepticism]]** about people who hold their categories too tightly
- **The [[SIGGRAPH]] window 1995–2010s** — the formation of physically-based rendering, fluid simulation, OpenVDB, the whole classical-procedural lineage
- **[[Donato Giancola]]'s atelier-rigor exemplar** in painting

… the demand for *multiple ways to examine the truth* is non-negotiable.

**Autobiographical evidence: the [[SIGGRAPH disengagement]].** Paul attended SIGGRAPH continuously from 1995 until "it became about hybrid approaches" — meaning AI/ML braided into every part of every pipeline (fluid sim with learned sub-grid behavior, multi-phase coupling with learned closure models). Classical methods didn't disappear; they got *colonized*. The room's vocabulary, intuitions, and evaluation criteria all shifted even when the underlying problem statements looked the same. Paul left because **the epistemic standards changed**, not because the topics did.

**A meaningful chunk of Paul's heritage is methodological, not aesthetic.** The illustration-and-painters axis is the visible, glamorous one. The methodological one — Pasteur, comparative religion, Theravada attention, classical-PBR-and-simulation rigor, the demand for examinability — is quieter but probably *stronger as a determinant of how Paul actually works*.

**Defining the current professional position:**

Paul's current work makes sense in this frame. **Synthetic data with intrinsic annotation and procedural ground truth is the rigorous, examinable answer to the same questions the hybrid-ML SIGGRAPH wave is answering with learned approximations.** Paul isn't anti-ML — he's providing the substrate that lets ML be done with epistemic seriousness. ["Building the Matrix"](Building the Matrix) is the thesis; the SIGGRAPH disengagement is the autobiographical evidence for why he holds it.

A meaningful chunk of Paul's heritage is partly defined by **what he's stayed loyal to as the field moved.** The classical procedural / simulation / PBR tradition is a living lineage Paul is still working in, and his current professional differentiation rests on that loyalty.
