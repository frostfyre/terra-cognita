# TV drama — MASH, Gilmore Girls, Game of Thrones, Sopranos

Four distinct television entries, each doing different work on the map.

## MASH — lifelong, shared with [[Chandra]]

**Specifically the post-Henry Blake era** — Colonel Potter, BJ Hunnicutt, Charles Winchester. The show's mature, morally serious period rather than the early broad comedy. The shift from ensemble-comedy to sustained humanist drama about people maintaining their humanity under conditions designed to strip it away.

**Lifelong fan, shared with Chandra.** This is a genuinely *theirs* entry — not Paul discovering something and Chandra joining, or vice versa, but a shared lifelong relationship with the show. The found-family-of-misfits-in-impossible-circumstances structure; the comedy and tragedy coexisting without canceling each other; the specific era Paul and Chandra prefer says something about which register they're drawn to — the moral seriousness rather than the broad humor.

## Gilmore Girls — shared with Chandra

Fast dialogue, mother-daughter relationship, small-town community as the world, wit and warmth. A different register from the rest of Paul's TV landscape — domestic, comedic, relationship-focused. But the rapid-fire-literary-reference dialogue and the sense of a community with real texture connects to the [[music-as-worldbuilding-substrate]] and [[place-as-character]] instincts operating in a domestic register.

## Game of Thrones

The high-fantasy-epic-with-real-consequence register. Long-arc worldbuilding, character death that sticks, moral complexity, the [[operatic-tragic-register]] at sustained television scale. The GRRM approach — Claremont-X-Men-level commitment to consequence — applied to fantasy television. The show's quality arc is itself a complicated heritage entry (the later seasons are a different aesthetic object from the earlier ones), but the early-to-mid seasons represent genuine achievement in sustained consequence storytelling.

## The Sopranos — watched with a neighbor

**The social context matters**: watched with a neighbor, which means communal viewing as part of the experience. The show is one of the great American television achievements — the protagonist-as-the-problem sustained for six seasons, the violence and domesticity genuinely intertwined, the [[operatic-tragic-register]] at its most morally complex. Tony Soprano is the definitive sustained-protagonist-who-is-the-problem in American television.

The neighbor detail is worth preserving as texture: shared viewing with community, the kind of social ritual around great television. Same spirit as the SCA and GameSchool Con — belonging to communities around shared experiences.

## What these add together

Across these four shows: **moral complexity sustained over time, found-family-under-pressure, the operatic-tragic register in domestic and genre contexts, and genuinely shared viewing with Chandra** (MASH, Gilmore Girls). The TV drama axis shows Paul drawn to long-arc work with real consequence — same instinct as the comics axis (Elfquest, Claremont X-Men) expressed in television form.
