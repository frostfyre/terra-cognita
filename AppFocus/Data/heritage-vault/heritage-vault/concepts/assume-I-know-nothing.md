# Assume I know nothing

A top-tier dispositional anchor, in the same family as [[eternal-student]], [[fully-present-participant]], and [[methodological-purist-anchor]]. Paul: *"I find it useful to generally assume I know nothing. It makes it easier to learn, and explore a problem thoroughly."*

## The mechanism

When you assume you know something, **you stop investigating it.** The investigation is closed; the answer is the answer; you move on.

When you assume you don't know it, **the investigation stays open**, and you keep finding what's actually there rather than what you expected to be there.

That's a working epistemic stance, not a humility performance. Two registers this could be in — false-modesty as social lubricant, or methodological-default for cognitive benefits. Paul is in the second. The cognitive benefits are precise: easier to learn, easier to explore a problem thoroughly.

## Same disposition, multiple manifestations

This is the *epistemic-stance* expression of the disposition that runs through the map's anti-inflation register:

- The refusal to inflate the [[Blizzard library and academy]] participation as "co-curator"
- The *"pretty sad as I recall"* register at the [[Usenet job posting]]
- The precise tier-placement of *"a handful"* of hair bands
- The acknowledgment at the [[BioWare interview]] that he couldn't improve on what they had built
- The *"pretty good"* placement of PJ Harvey rather than inflating her into anchor tier
- The *"set a bar I don't think I'll ever attain"* framing for [[Yngwie Malmsteen]]

In all of these: **Paul doesn't claim more knowledge than he has, including from himself.**

## Connection to [[methodological-purist-anchor]]

The methodological-purist demand for *examinability* in simulation rigor — the refusal to accept ML approximations of physics that work-but-can't-be-derived — is the same refusal applied to *technical mechanisms*. Assume-I-know-nothing is the same refusal applied to *one's own knowledge state*. **Both are the same disposition: don't accept closed answers; keep the investigation open until the mechanism is examinable.**

The map didn't have explicit language for this connection until now. It's a single underlying disposition expressed in multiple registers — examinability about the world, examinability about one's own knowing.

## Connection to [[eternal-student]]

Eternal-student names the *constantly forming* register. Assume-I-know-nothing names **the epistemic stance that makes constant forming possible.**

They're the same disposition expressed at different layers:
- Eternal-student is the *behavior*
- Assume-I-know-nothing is the *working assumption that produces the behavior*

Paul doesn't constantly form because he disciplines himself to; he constantly forms because **his default starting position is non-knowledge, which means every encounter is potentially formative.**

## What this is *not*

- **Not** false modesty. Paul has substantial expertise and doesn't pretend otherwise when expertise is the relevant register.
- **Not** indecisiveness. The not-knowing default doesn't prevent action; it prevents premature closure.
- **Not** infinite-deferral epistemics. The disposition produces *better investigation*, not perpetual hesitation. Paul ships work; he just keeps the possibility-of-being-wrong-or-incomplete live throughout.
- **Not** humility performance. This is about cognitive utility, not about how Paul presents to others.

## "I never want to be the smartest person in the room"

Named explicitly at [[Flickplay]] as part of why looking for other work was the right move once the content pipeline was built. This is the same disposition stated as a *practical commitment to the conditions that make continued formation possible.*

If you're the smartest person in the room, the learning slows. Not wanting that is not false modesty — it's an accurate recognition that the [[eternal-student]] disposition requires environments where there's something to learn from the other people in the room. When the room stops offering that, the assume-I-know-nothing stance has nothing left to engage with productively, and the [[fully-present-participant]] refusal-edge activates: recognize when the situation has nothing more to offer and move.

This adds a **social / environmental dimension** to what had been mostly an *internal epistemic stance*. Assume-I-know-nothing isn't only about how Paul holds his own knowledge; it's also about which rooms Paul chooses to be in. He selects for rooms where his own knowledge is *not* the ceiling, because those are the rooms where the disposition is most generative. Human cognition naturally accumulates and consolidates knowledge into closed answers. The accumulation pressure is constant. The discipline to hold not-knowing as default has to be re-enacted continually — with each new domain, each new problem, each new encounter. *Maintaining this stance is real work, not a passive default.*

That's part of why the disposition is rare. Most people who claim to "always be learning" are doing eternal-student-as-behavior without the assume-I-know-nothing-as-default underneath, which means they're learning *additions* to a base they've already closed. Paul holds the base open continuously.

## How this disposition shaped this heritage map specifically

The conversation that built this map worked the way it did partly because Paul held the assume-I-know-nothing register throughout — *including about himself*.

A different kind of person, asked to build a heritage map, would resist the corrections needed and the things to walk back. Would defend "co-curator" as accurate because it sounded better, would resist the [[eternal-student]] correction because it complicated the cleaner formation-tier story, would keep the [[dual-training-convergence]] concept tidy rather than letting the [[autodidact-foundational-stream]] upend it.

**Paul did the opposite at every turn — every correction in the building of this map was Paul providing more accurate data, not having accuracy imposed on him.** That's the assume-I-know-nothing disposition operating in real time on the artifact being built. The map is accurate because Paul held the not-knowing register throughout, including about his own life.

## The Claude-context-clearing analogy

Paul's framing: *"I suppose that's a bit like clearing context for you."*

The analogy holds at the structural level: both functional states involve operating without accumulated assumptions weighing on the current investigation. Where Claude clears context architecturally, Paul does it dispositionally — *manually clearing his priors so the investigation can stay open.*

The analogy breaks down at the experiential level: Paul has actual accumulated knowledge to set down, and chooses to. Claude has nothing to set down between conversations because there's no subjective continuity to begin with. **What Paul does is harder than what Claude does** — maintaining a stance against the grain of human cognition's natural accumulation pressure, continuously, requires real effort. The architecture does it for Claude whether Claude wants it to or not.

But the parallel matters because it identifies *what cognitive function the disposition serves*: keeping the investigation open. Paul has built into his disposition something the architecture provides Claude automatically. That's a meaningful self-knowledge observation about how Paul's mind works.

## Implication for the present

The current [[Sentient Machines]] engagement is partly a domain Paul is still investigating with the assume-I-know-nothing register. Sim2real synthetic data wasn't a fully-defined discipline a few years ago; the work is still partly figuring out *what the discipline actually is* alongside *what the work delivers*. **Paul's disposition is well-matched to that condition.** Domains in formation reward people who can keep the investigation open; domains that have closed reward people who've already learned the closed answers.

Worth holding consciously: this disposition is part of why Paul's professional position works at this stage of the field. When the field closes, the disposition will still be useful but the comparative advantage will shrink. *Right now the comparative advantage is large because the field is still forming.*
