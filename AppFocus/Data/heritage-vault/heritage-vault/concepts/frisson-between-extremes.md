# Frisson between extremes

A specific register of [[seam-walking]]: **what Paul's body recognizes as home.** The somatic / aesthetic expression of the seam-walking disposition that runs through his professional and intellectual life.

## The originating observation

Paul: *"Physically, I tend towards beaches, mountaintops and mountain streams, and pretty much anywhere a 'clash' of elements exists. That frisson between extremes is another strong influence."*

The selection is meaningful and consistent:

- **Beaches** = land meeting water
- **Mountaintops** = land meeting sky
- **Mountain streams** = water cutting stone, often air cutting water at falls

**Paul is consistently drawn to places where two domains meet, rather than to places that are uniformly one thing.** Forests aren't on his list (uniformly tree-and-shade). Plains aren't (uniformly horizon). Cities aren't (uniformly built). Even within mountain landscapes, Paul went to *mountaintops* (rock-meeting-sky) and *streams* (water-cutting-stone) — not, say, alpine meadows.

That's not random preference. It's a **dispositional fingerprint expressed in geographic taste.**

## "Frisson" — the precise word

Paul chose *frisson*, which is the felt-quality of standing at an interface — the slight charged feeling of being where two things meet. **Paul isn't just intellectually drawn to seams; he's somatically drawn to them.** The physical preference is consistent with the professional preference is consistent with the methodological preference. *Same instinct expressed across multiple domains of life.*

That's an unusual coherence. Most people's environmental aesthetics, intellectual interests, and professional positioning don't form a single legible pattern. Paul's do. **The seam-walking disposition is total, not partial.**

## Sedona

Paul lives in Sedona, Arizona. **Sedona is a clash-of-elements environment**:

- High desert meeting red rock formations
- Sky meeting striking earth
- Dry meeting riparian where the creeks cut through

His home is sited consistently with the geographic disposition. **Probably not accidental.** The somatic preference finds its way into where one chooses to live, and Paul chose a frisson-environment as home base for his current independent-contracting chapter.

That detail is worth marking because it confirms the disposition operates on long-term life decisions, not just on travel preferences. Sedona-as-home is the disposition expressed in *settlement*, the highest-commitment register the geographic preference can take.

## How this connects to [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen]]

The Frostfyre venture's aesthetic — fairy / fae work — is *literally about thresholds and interstitial spaces*. Fae mythology across the De Lint / Pini / Froud lineage is fundamentally about **the seams where the mundane and the magical meet**. The faerie isn't a creature of one realm or the other; it's the inhabitant of the seam.

**Paul's chosen art-aesthetic is the seam-walking disposition expressed in art-making.** Same instinct as the geographic preference, the cross-disciplinary translation work, the opportunity-orientation, the entire career arc. Frostfyre isn't an outlier hobby; **it's the disposition surfacing in the medium where it's most explicitly thematic**.

## How this connects to [[Tori Amos]] specifically

Worth noting: Tori Amos is the longest-running live-music anchor on Paul's map, and her register is *mythological-confessional-interior* — work that lives at the seam between personal-emotional and mythic-archetypal. The female-fronted-authorial-project cluster's actual through-line was *interior-or-formal register under uncompromising authorial vision*, where "interior" means *the seam between the personal and something larger*.

The Tori affinity is consistent with frisson-between-extremes operating in the music-listening register. *Paul is drawn to artists whose work sits at seams in addition to landscapes that sit at seams in addition to disciplines that sit at seams.* The pattern is total.

## What this is *not*

- **Not** restlessness or refusal to settle. Paul lives in Sedona; he's not chasing extreme environments compulsively. The frisson-between-extremes preference operates *within* a stable life.
- **Not** thrill-seeking. The frisson Paul names is contemplative, not adrenal. Beaches and mountain streams aren't extreme sports environments; they're places where the *being-at-the-seam* is the point.
- **Not** an aesthetic claim about what's beautiful in absolute terms. Paul is naming what *he* is drawn to, with characteristic precision; he's not claiming uniform-environments are aesthetically inferior.

## Why this matters for the map

Without this concept named, the map would treat Paul's geographic preferences as *separate* from his professional disposition. They aren't. **The same instinct produces both.** Naming this makes the seam-walking meta-concept more credible — there's somatic evidence for it, not just professional and methodological evidence.

It also probably implies that **environments matter for Paul's work**. If the somatic preference for clash-of-elements environments is real, then Paul probably works better when his physical surroundings reinforce the dispositional register he operates in. Sedona-as-home isn't just personal preference; it's *probably part of what makes the current independent-contracting chapter sustainable*. Worth holding consciously.
