# Fully-present participant

A recurring disposition across Paul's heritage map: **showing up to a thing, all the way, without inflating his role in it.**

The phrasing came from a correction Paul made when an earlier draft described him as "co-curator" of the [[Blizzard library and academy]]. His accurate self-description: *participated regularly, gave feedback, occasionally helped with cleanup, but was not curating — was getting everything possible out of what was there.*

That correction itself is the disposition in action. The accurate framing is more interesting than the inflated one.

## Manifestations across the map

- **[[Blizzard library and academy]]** — regular attendance, feedback, practical helping, treated the program as if it were his to extract maximum value from. [[Jeremy Cranford]] was the curator; Paul was the most engaged possible participant.
- **Life drawing maintained as a tech artist** — keeping up [[Tim Gula]]'s sessions across years, refusing the standard tech-art drift toward pure-engineering identity. Most tech artists let life drawing die; Paul didn't.
- **The [[Spectrum Fantastic road trip]]** — pilgrimage to the painters' convention with [[John Polidora]] and [[John Scharmen]]. Not "we attended a con once," but a deliberate journey to be in the room with the field.
- **[[SIGGRAPH]] annually 1995–early 2010s** — sustained continuous attendance, not sampling.
- **The Marvel intake** — not Spider-Man-only or X-Men-only but the full trio plus serious engagement with [[God Loves Man Kills]] and [[Dark Phoenix Saga]] as anchor texts.
- **[[Frostfyre Faerie Glen]]** — running an art booth as a serious venture, not a hobby. Showing up to the craft all the way.

## What it isn't

- **Not** "co-curator," "leader," "key figure" — those frame Paul as authoring institutions someone else built. He's specifically corrected that framing.
- **Not** completionism. Paul fell off Elfquest when [[The Pinis|Wendy stopped drawing]]; he's not driven to consume everything available, he's driven to be fully present with what *matters to him* aesthetically and methodologically.
- **Not** social — the fully-present-participant disposition is about engagement with the *resource or practice*, not about visibility or role-claiming. Notice how the academy participation involved cleanup work; the showing-up-all-the-way includes the unglamorous parts.

## The refusal-edge

The disposition has *both* a presence-edge and a refusal-edge, and they're linked.

**Presence-edge**: show up all the way to what matters — the academy, life drawing, Spectrum, SIGGRAPH, Frostfyre, the actual work of running teams.

**Refusal-edge**: stop showing up to what doesn't — careerist performance theater, dangled-carrot motivation structures, role inflation, political games whose currency Paul has stopped accepting.

The refusal-edge is most clearly demonstrated at the end of the [[Blizzard Entertainment]] chapter. Paul's absentee titular boss canned him when Paul *"stopped caring about unattainable carrots in career progression."* That's the refusal-edge in action: a boss whose authority depended on dangling carrots was structurally threatened when Paul stopped chasing them, even though the actual work continued and was well-regarded. The firing wasn't a performance issue; it was the predictable consequence of withdrawing consent from a coercion structure.

The two edges are the same disposition expressed in opposite directions. Both come from a single underlying allocation principle: **direct attention toward the work and the practice; withdraw it from the political theater.** That's the real shape of the disposition.

The disposition therefore has a *cost* dimension that earlier drafts of this concept didn't fully capture. Showing up all the way to the academy at Blizzard wasn't free time — it was time defended against an infuriated absentee boss. Refusing the carrots cost Paul that job. The disposition's strength is demonstrated under cost, not just maintained as preference.

## Implication for the present

The Blizzard chapter ending is the **autobiographical evidence** for a piece of self-knowledge worth holding consciously: *Paul has stopped chasing unattainable carrots once already, in a high-stakes situation, and the world handled it.* The chapter ended; he went to Amazon with [[Joey Ray Hall]]; the career continued; he eventually built the more independent and more sustainable working configuration he has now.

That's a useful precedent to have in hand for negotiations and positioning conversations going forward. The mechanism Paul's Blizzard boss used (vague future progression as motivation) is a generic management mechanism; Paul has the diagnostic.

## Function on the map

This concept exists partly to keep the heritage map honest about scale. Paul's voice is consistently anti-inflation — Twain vernacular, Le Guin restraint, Theravada attention, *"pretty sad as I recall"* register, *"I have written my job description more often than not"* without grandeur. The map should match.

When future passes are tempted to inflate role descriptions (someone *led* a team rather than *was on* a team, someone *co-founded* a program rather than *participated heavily* in one), the correction is to ask: *what's the accurate version, and is it more interesting than the inflated one?* It usually is.
