# Bar-setter acknowledged as unreachable but admired

A function-tag that surfaced explicitly via the [[Yngwie Malmsteen]] entry but applies retroactively to several figures already on the map. **Figures whose excellences are not Paul's, whose specific medium-skill he doesn't try to embody, but whose standards calibrate his sense of what craft-in-general looks like.**

## Definition

Three properties define the function:

1. **The figure's excellence is in a domain Paul doesn't work in personally** — guitar virtuosity for [[Yngwie Malmsteen]], academic painting for [[Donato Giancola]], composition-and-lighting at illustration scale for [[Wei Wang]], line-economy for [[Moebius]].
2. **Paul honestly acknowledges the limit** — *"set a bar I don't think I'll ever attain, but admire intensely"* is the prototype phrase. No pretense of approaching the bar; no dismissal of the bar's value. Mature relationship to excellence that exceeds his reach.
3. **The standard travels across disciplines even when the specific skill doesn't.** The bar Paul can't attain on guitar (or in painting, or in line-drawing) becomes calibration for *what bar-setting looks like* — applied to his own work in tech-art, simulation, and synthetic data.

## Why this function matters distinctly

This is *not* the same as **influence** (figures who shape Paul's own practice) or **aesthetic anchor** (figures whose work Paul returns to as touchstones). It's a third category: figures who are **calibration-for-rigor** without being templates.

Most maps of artistic heritage collapse this category into "influence" or "favorite artist." Keeping it distinct surfaces something specific about Paul's relationship to excellence: **he can hold a standard he can't personally meet, in a discipline he doesn't practice, as a live reference point for his own work in a different discipline.** That's a portable disposition. It explains why the [[methodological-purist-anchor]] streak is consistent across his work even though his technical formation runs through tech-art rather than through any of the specific traditions whose rigor he admires.

## Confirmed entries

- **[[Yngwie Malmsteen]]** — virtuosity-as-its-own-criterion, neoclassical-classical-grounding-under-surface-flash. The prototype.

## Probable retroactive entries (worth tagging)

- **[[Donato Giancola]]** — atelier rigor in painting
- **[[James Gurney]]** — observational discipline applied to imaginative realism
- **[[Wei Wang]]** — composition and dramatic lighting at illustration scale
- **[[Moebius]]** — line economy and architectural imagination
- **[[Steve Huston]]** — figure construction in the Bridgman lineage
- **[[Bob Watts]]** — Reilly-method atelier rigor

## What this function isn't

- **Not** envy. Paul's relationship to these figures is admiration without destabilization.
- **Not** aspiration in the sense of "I'm trying to become this." Paul is not trying to be a guitar virtuoso, an academic painter, or an atelier-trained draughtsman.
- **Not** tourism. Paul takes the standards seriously enough that they actually calibrate his work in adjacent disciplines.

## Connection to broader map

The bar-setter function is one of the quiet engines of [[methodological-purist-anchor]]. Paul's purism in simulation work isn't built only from his own technical formation — it's also built from *standards held across disciplines*, including ones he doesn't practice. The painters and the virtuoso musician calibrate his sense of what *rigor done seriously* looks like in any medium. The synthetic data pipelines benefit from the same standards that define Yngwie's neoclassical phrasing or Donato's value structure.
