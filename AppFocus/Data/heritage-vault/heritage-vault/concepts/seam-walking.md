# Seam-walking

A meta-disposition that runs across cognitive, methodological, and somatic registers in Paul's life. **Paul is consistently drawn to seams** — places where two domains meet rather than places that are uniformly one thing.

This concept is the *frame* that three more specific concepts extend:

- [[opportunity-orientation]] — how Paul moves toward seams
- [[cross-disciplinary-translation-with-trap-awareness]] — how Paul operates when at a seam
- [[frisson-between-extremes]] — what Paul's body recognizes as home

Each of those is the seam-walking disposition expressed in a different register. The meta-concept exists because the three together are more coherent than any one alone, and naming the underlying instinct keeps the map honest about what's actually one disposition rather than three coincidentally related ones.

## Why the disposition coheres

A seam offers what the interior of a single domain doesn't: **two different vocabularies, two different toolsets, two different evaluation criteria, available simultaneously to whoever can hold both.** Most people specialize and become fluent in one domain's interior; the seam-walker stays at the boundary where translation is required and possible.

That's harder than specialization in two specific ways:

- **Cognitively** — holding two frameworks in working memory and noticing where they almost-but-not-quite match (see [[cross-disciplinary-translation-with-trap-awareness]])
- **Socially** — being illegible from inside either single domain, since each domain's specialists usually can't see the seam-walker's full work (see [[peer-credentialed-rather-than-consumer-credentialed]])

It's also *where novel work tends to live*. New disciplines often form at seams — sim2real synthetic data is itself a seam between procedural rendering and ML training; tech-art was originally a seam between artists and engineers that propagated into a discipline. **Paul's career has consistently been operative at seams that later became disciplines, partly because seam-walking is what he does naturally.**

## Why naming this matters for the map

Without this meta-concept, the three sub-concepts ([[opportunity-orientation]], [[cross-disciplinary-translation-with-trap-awareness]], [[frisson-between-extremes]]) would look like related-but-separate dispositions. They aren't separate; they're the same instinct surfacing in different registers.

The meta-concept also explains *why several already-named patterns in the map cohere*:

- The [[autodidact-foundational-stream]] is forward-investment study at *the seam where rendering algorithms meet procedural thinking* — both later became disciplines, but Paul was studying their seam before either was settled.
- The [[dual-training-convergence]] is the convergence of *Blizzard visual-craft and Amazon software-and-ML* — Paul didn't get either training in pure form; he got each in registers that already-bridged toward the other ([[Chris Adams]] as software-engineering-applied-to-tech-art is itself a seam-positioned teacher).
- The [[Frostfyre Faerie Glen]] register works because fae art *is* a seam aesthetic; the venture sits naturally in Paul's broader disposition rather than being a hobbyist outlier.

**The map is more coherent with this concept named than without it.**

## The home-in-Sedona observation

Paul lives in Sedona, Arizona — a *clash-of-elements environment*: high desert meeting red rock formations, sky meeting striking earth, dry meeting riparian where the creeks cut through. **His home is sited consistently with the seam-walking disposition.** Probably not accidental. The somatic preference (see [[frisson-between-extremes]]) finds its way into where one chooses to live.
