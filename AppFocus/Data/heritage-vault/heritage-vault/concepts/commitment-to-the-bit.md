# Commitment to the bit

Take the premise as seriously as the emotion. Don't wink at the audience. Push the conceit through to its logical conclusion without apologizing for the register you've chosen.

Instances: [[Chuck Jones]] (Bugs Bunny playing fully within the cartoon logic), [[Joss Whedon|Dr. Horrible]] (musical supervillain tragedy played straight), [[lion-in-winter-my-fair-lady|Moulin Rouge]] (operatic romance committed completely), [[Scott Pilgrim]] (video-game logic as real physics), [[Andy Warhol Interview Magazine|Warhol]] (Campbell's soup as fine art, run until undeniable).

The opposite of ironic distance. Not naive — the sophistication is in the commitment, not in the winking acknowledgment that it's silly.
