# Betterman

The cognitive structure Phil Betterman embodies in [[Chris Sanders]]'s *The Croods: A New Age* (2020) — Paul reads this as a tech-bro parable. See [[Chris Sanders]] for the full treatment.

**The core pattern:** optimization without integration; sophistication without reciprocity; the certainty that you've figured it out. Named for a family whose surname is the joke — *Better*man — because they believe they are.

**The Betterman failure mode:** systems that are efficient but brittle, that curate out the complexity they don't understand rather than engaging with it, that create dependencies disguised as solutions.

**The anti-Betterman dispositions on the map:** [[assume-I-know-nothing]], [[eternal-student]], [[fully-present-participant]]. The person who knows they haven't figured it out is structurally protected from Bettermanism.

**Connects to:** [[Chuck Jones]] (suits-in-the-room as Bettermanism applied to creative institutions), [[Sentient Machines]] (domain randomization as the explicit anti-curation move — ship the chaos, not the curated dataset).
