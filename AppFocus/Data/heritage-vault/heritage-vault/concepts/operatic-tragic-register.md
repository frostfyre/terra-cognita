# Operatic tragic register

A recurring aesthetic pattern across Paul's formation — works that commit fully to large emotional stakes, where the tragedy is inherent to the structure rather than avoidable, and where the watching/reading is the point rather than the resolution.

Instances across the map: [[Michael Moorcock|Elric]], [[Dark Phoenix Saga]], [[TV drama — MASH etc|The Sopranos]], [[lion-in-winter-my-fair-lady|The Lion in Winter]], [[Joss Whedon|Dr. Horrible]], [[akira-nge-lodoss-war|Neon Genesis Evangelion]], [[Game of Thrones]].

Distinct from tragedy-as-punishment (Greek hubris model). Closer to: the thing being beautiful *because* it cannot be sustained; the watching being the point; the emotional register large enough to contain the full weight of what's being depicted without apologizing for it.

Connects to:: [[protagonist-as-the-problem]] (the tragic register often involves the protagonist's nature as the mechanism)
