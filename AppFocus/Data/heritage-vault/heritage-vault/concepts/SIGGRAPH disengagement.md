# SIGGRAPH disengagement

Paul attended [[SIGGRAPH]] annually from 1995 through the early 2010s, then disengaged when AI/ML colonized the conference and it stopped being the room it used to be. Same pattern as [[San Diego Comic-Con]] disengagement: accurate recognition that the room had changed, not hostility to the new direction.
