# Eternal Champion

[[Michael Moorcock]]'s multiverse concept — the same soul appearing across multiple realities in different forms (Elric, Hawkmoon, Corum, Jerry Cornelius et al). Each incarnation different but recognizably the same essential consciousness. The multiverse as a structural principle; identity persisting across incompatible realities. Also appears in [[chobits-bondage-fairies-tsubasa|Tsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle]] as a parallel structure.
