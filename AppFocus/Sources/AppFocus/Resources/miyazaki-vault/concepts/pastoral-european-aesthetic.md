# Pastoral European aesthetic

Cobbled streets, alpine meadows, half-timbered houses, Mediterranean ports. [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s 1971 [[Astrid Lindgren]] research trip across Sweden — for an adaptation that never happened — left a stock of imagery that resurfaces across decades.
