# Diana Wynne Jones

British author of *Howl's Moving Castle* (1986), the novel [[Hayao Miyazaki]] adapted into [[Howls Moving Castle]] in 2004. She reportedly approved of the film despite its substantial divergence from the book, and is said to have appreciated that Miyazaki understood the *spirit* of the story even as he changed its surface.
