# Eiko Kadono

Japanese children's author of *Kiki's Delivery Service* (1985), adapted by [[Hayao Miyazaki]] into [[Kikis Delivery Service]] in 1989. The adaptation famously bent the book's structure considerably, which initially upset Kadono before she came around to it.
