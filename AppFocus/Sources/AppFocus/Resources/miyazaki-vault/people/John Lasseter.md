# John Lasseter

American animator, co-founder of Pixar, and longtime [[Hayao Miyazaki]] admirer. Lasseter championed Ghibli films in the United States in the late 1990s and 2000s; the [[Disney]] English-language dubs of [[Princess Mononoke]] and [[Spirited Away]] were shepherded through his offices.
