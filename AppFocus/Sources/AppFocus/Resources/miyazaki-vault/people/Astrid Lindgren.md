# Astrid Lindgren

Swedish author of *Pippi Longstocking*. In 1971, [[Hayao Miyazaki]] and [[Isao Takahata]] travelled to Sweden to research a Pippi adaptation that ultimately collapsed when Lindgren declined the rights. The trip's imagery — Swedish landscapes, Stockholm, the [[pastoral-european-aesthetic|European pastoral]] — would surface a decade later in [[Kikis Delivery Service]] and elsewhere.
