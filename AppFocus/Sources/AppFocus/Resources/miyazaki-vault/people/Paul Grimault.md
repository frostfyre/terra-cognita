# Paul Grimault

French animator whose *Le Roi et l'oiseau* (1980, recut from an unfinished 1952 production) [[Hayao Miyazaki]] has named as one of the films that most marked him. The towering vertical castle, the flying birds, the tone of melancholy mechanical fantasy — all surface in [[Castle in the Sky]] and elsewhere.

influenced:: [[Hayao Miyazaki]]
