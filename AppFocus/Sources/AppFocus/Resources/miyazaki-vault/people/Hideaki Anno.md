# Hideaki Anno

Animator and director, best known outside this graph for *Neon Genesis Evangelion*. Inside it, he is the wild-eyed young animator [[Hayao Miyazaki]] handed the "God Warrior" sequence in [[Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind]] in 1984 — one of the most technically demanding scenes in the film. He returned to [[Studio Ghibli]] decades later to voice the lead in [[The Wind Rises]].
