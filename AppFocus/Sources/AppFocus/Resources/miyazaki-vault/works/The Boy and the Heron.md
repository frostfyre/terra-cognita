# The Boy and the Heron

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]], released after a decade of work and a famously hands-off marketing campaign — [[Toshio Suzuki]] released essentially no trailers in Japan. Won the [[Academy Award]] for Best Animated Feature in 2024, [[spirited-away-oscar|Miyazaki's second]]. A late-career synthesis: [[children-as-protagonists|a grieving boy]], a moving castle of dreams, and an old man weighing his succession.
