# Tales from Earthsea

Directed by [[Goro Miyazaki]] — his directorial debut, made over the public objections of his father [[Hayao Miyazaki]]. Based loosely on Ursula K. Le Guin's Earthsea cycle. The fallout between father and son around this production was severe and later repaired.
