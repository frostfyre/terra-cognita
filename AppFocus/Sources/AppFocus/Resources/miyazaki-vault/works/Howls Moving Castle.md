# Howl's Moving Castle

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]], adapted from [[Diana Wynne Jones]]'s 1986 novel. The wartime backdrop and bombing imagery are Miyazaki interventions, not Wynne Jones's — a [[pacifism-anti-war|Miyazaki signature]] grafted onto the source. Score by [[Joe Hisaishi]].
