# Ponyo

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]]. A return to hand-drawn-only animation after the heavier CG of [[Howls Moving Castle]] — every frame, including the waves, drawn by hand. The film leans on [[children-as-protagonists|two five-year-olds]] and a fish-girl with the moral force of the ocean.
