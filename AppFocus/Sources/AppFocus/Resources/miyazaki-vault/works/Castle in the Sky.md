# Castle in the Sky

The first official [[Studio Ghibli]] film. Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]]; debt to [[Paul Grimault]]'s *Le Roi et l'oiseau* is visible in the floating castle and bird imagery. Score by [[Joe Hisaishi]] — the theme remains one of his most-played pieces.
