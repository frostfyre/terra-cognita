# Porco Rosso

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]]; an Adriatic-set interwar fable about a seaplane pilot cursed into the shape of a pig. The film is Miyazaki at his most personal — a middle-aged man retreating to an island to fix his own aircraft, surrounded by women who keep him alive. Score by [[Joe Hisaishi]].
