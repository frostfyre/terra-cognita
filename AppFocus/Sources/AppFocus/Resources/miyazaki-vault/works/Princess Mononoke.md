# Princess Mononoke

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]] and produced by [[Toshio Suzuki]]. The film's ecological violence — gun-foundry villagers, wolf gods, the dying forest — pushed Ghibli's reputation into wider international territory. [[Disney]]'s English dub, shepherded by [[John Lasseter]], opened the studio's American distribution era.
