# Lupin III: The Castle of Cagliostro

[[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s feature directorial debut, made before [[Studio Ghibli]] existed. A heist caper set in a fictional European microstate; the chase choreography and improbable architecture announce the Miyazaki camera in embryonic form.
