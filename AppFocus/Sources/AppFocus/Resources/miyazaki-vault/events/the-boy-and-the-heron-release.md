# The Boy and the Heron release

[[The Boy and the Heron]] opened in Japan in July 2023 with one of the most restrained marketing campaigns in modern film history: [[Toshio Suzuki]] declined to release trailers domestically, leaving audiences to enter the film cold. It won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in March 2024.
