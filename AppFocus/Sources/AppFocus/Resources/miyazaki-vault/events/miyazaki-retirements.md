# Miyazaki's retirements

[[Hayao Miyazaki]] has publicly retired multiple times since the early 2000s, most notably after [[The Wind Rises]] in 2013. Each retirement has been followed, eventually, by a return — most recently [[The Boy and the Heron]] in 2023.
