# Future Boy Conan

[[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s first solo directing credit — a 26-episode TV series for NHK based loosely on Alexander Key's novel *The Incredible Tide*. The post-apocalyptic island setting, the flying machines, the [[strong-female-protagonists|young heroine Lana]] all anticipate [[Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind]].
