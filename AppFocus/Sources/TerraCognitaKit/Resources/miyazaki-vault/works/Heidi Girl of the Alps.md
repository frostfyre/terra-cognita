# Heidi, Girl of the Alps

A 52-episode TV adaptation directed by [[Isao Takahata]] with [[Hayao Miyazaki]] as scene designer and layout artist. Made in the wake of the abandoned Pippi project that had sent the two to Europe in 1971. The series's [[pastoral-european-aesthetic|alpine pastoral]] became foundational to both men's visual language.
