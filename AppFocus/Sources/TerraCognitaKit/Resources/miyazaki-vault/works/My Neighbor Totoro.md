# My Neighbor Totoro

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]] and released as a double feature with [[Isao Takahata]]'s [[Grave of the Fireflies]] — a release pairing now legendary for its tonal whiplash. The film's central pastoral aesthetic and animist forest spirits became the studio's visual signature.
