# Grave of the Fireflies

Directed by [[Isao Takahata]] for [[Studio Ghibli]] in 1988, released as a double feature with [[My Neighbor Totoro]]. A wartime story of two orphaned siblings starving in 1945 Kobe — perhaps the most devastating [[pacifism-anti-war|anti-war]] animated film ever made.
