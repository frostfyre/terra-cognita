# The Wind Rises

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]]; a biographical fiction about aeronautical engineer Jiro Horikoshi, designer of the Zero fighter. The film triangulates [[Hayao Miyazaki|Miyazaki]]'s lifelong [[flying-machines|love of aircraft]] with his [[pacifism-anti-war|pacifism]] and the moral compromise of building beautiful things for terrible ends. Voiced in the lead by [[Hideaki Anno]].
