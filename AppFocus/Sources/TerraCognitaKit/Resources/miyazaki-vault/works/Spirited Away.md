# Spirited Away

Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]], produced by [[Toshio Suzuki]]. Won [[spirited-away-oscar|the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature]] in 2003 and remained Japan's highest-grossing film for nearly two decades. A 10-year-old [[children-as-protagonists|girl]] navigates a bathhouse of spirits — the film is a compendium of [[animism-shinto|kami]] iconography.
