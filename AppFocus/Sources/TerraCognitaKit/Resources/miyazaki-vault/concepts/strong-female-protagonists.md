# Strong female protagonists

The Miyazaki heroine: capable, morally clear, often very young, almost never defined by romance with a male lead. Nausicaä, Sheeta, San, Sophie, Chihiro, Kiki — each carries the film. [[Hayao Miyazaki]] has said in interviews that he simply finds women more interesting to draw and to write.
