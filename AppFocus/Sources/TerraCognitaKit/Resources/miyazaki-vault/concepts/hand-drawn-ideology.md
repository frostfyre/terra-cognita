# Hand-drawn animation as ideology

[[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s public hostility to digital shortcut is sharp enough to be a stance, not a preference. [[Ponyo]] was made entirely by hand including the wave animation. The studio under his leadership has used digital tools sparingly and only as augmentation of pencil-and-cel work.

embodied-by:: [[Studio Ghibli]]
expressed-in:: [[Ponyo]]
