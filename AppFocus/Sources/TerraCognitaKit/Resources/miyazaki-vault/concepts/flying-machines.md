# Flying machines

A motif so consistent across [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s work that it functions as authorial signature. Gliders, biplanes, broomsticks, dragons, walking castles, fish-girls riding waves — almost every film features at least one mode of impossible-but-mechanically-rigorous flight, often piloted by a young protagonist.

The technical specificity comes from his lifelong love of aviation; his father ran a factory that made parts for the Zero, which [[The Wind Rises]] later confronted.
