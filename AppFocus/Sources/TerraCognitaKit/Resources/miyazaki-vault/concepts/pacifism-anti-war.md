# Pacifism / anti-war

A through-line strong enough to push [[Hayao Miyazaki]] into public political stances rare for a working director. [[Princess Mononoke]] refuses easy villains; [[Howls Moving Castle]] grafts a bombing campaign onto its source novel; [[The Wind Rises]] sits squarely on the moral horns of building beautiful aircraft for an aggressive war. [[Grave of the Fireflies]] is [[Isao Takahata]]'s contribution to the same conviction.
