# Osamu Tezuka

"God of manga", whose work and studio Mushi Production cast a long shadow over post-war Japanese animation. [[Hayao Miyazaki]] famously cited Tezuka as a formative influence but has been publicly ambivalent about the limited-animation techniques Tezuka's TV anime popularised — an ambivalence that shaped [[hand-drawn-ideology|his commitment to fuller animation]].
