# Toshio Suzuki

Producer, editor, and the third co-founder of [[Studio Ghibli]]. Came to animation through [[Tokuma Shoten]]'s anime magazine *Animage*, where he serialised [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s [[Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind]] manga. From the early 1980s onward he has been the producer of nearly every Miyazaki and [[Isao Takahata]] feature.

The studio's instinct for what to release, when, and how — and how to keep two demanding auteur directors funded for forty years — is largely his.
