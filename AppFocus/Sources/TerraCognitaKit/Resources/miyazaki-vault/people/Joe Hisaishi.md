# Joe Hisaishi

Composer of nearly every [[Hayao Miyazaki]] film score from [[Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind]] onward. His piano-driven, modally simple themes — "One Summer's Day", "Merry-Go-Round of Life", "Path of the Wind" — are inseparable from the films they accompany.

The collaboration shaped both careers: Miyazaki's films are remembered in part as Hisaishi melodies, and Hisaishi's international concert career is built largely on that catalogue.
