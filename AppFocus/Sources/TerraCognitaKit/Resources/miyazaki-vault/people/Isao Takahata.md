# Isao Takahata

Animator, director, and the closer of the two co-founders of [[Studio Ghibli]] when measured against [[Hayao Miyazaki]] himself. Met Miyazaki at [[Toei Animation]] in the 1960s; the two would shape each other's careers and the studio's identity for the next fifty years.

Where Miyazaki worked in the language of fantasy, Takahata's films tend toward neorealism, grief, and historical specificity — [[Grave of the Fireflies]] being the canonical example. He directed [[Heidi Girl of the Alps]] (with Miyazaki as scene designer) in 1974, a TV series whose [[pastoral-european-aesthetic|alpine pastoral imagery]] would echo through both men's later work.
