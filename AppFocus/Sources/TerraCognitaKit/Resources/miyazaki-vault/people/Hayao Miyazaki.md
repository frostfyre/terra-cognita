# Hayao Miyazaki

Japanese animator, director, manga artist, and co-founder of [[Studio Ghibli]]. Started his career at [[Toei Animation]] in 1963, where he met lifelong collaborator [[Isao Takahata]]. Across six decades he has written, directed, and animated films defined by flight, pacifism, animism, and resolute hand-drawn craft.

His authorial obsessions — [[flying-machines]], [[strong-female-protagonists]], [[animism-shinto]], and [[pacifism-anti-war]] — recur across nearly every film. He is famously hostile to digital shortcut, an avowed [[hand-drawn-ideology|believer in hand-drawn animation]], and has "retired" publicly multiple times only to return for one more film.
