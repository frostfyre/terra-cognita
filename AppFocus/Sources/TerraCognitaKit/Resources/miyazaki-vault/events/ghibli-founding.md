# Founding of Studio Ghibli

In June 1985, on the back of [[Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind]]'s success, [[Hayao Miyazaki]], [[Isao Takahata]], and [[Toshio Suzuki]] founded [[Studio Ghibli]] under the corporate cover of [[Tokuma Shoten]]. The name was Miyazaki's: an Italian colloquialism for a hot Saharan wind, repurposed as the name of a studio that would so often turn on currents of air.
