# Disney

The Walt Disney Company, distributor of [[Studio Ghibli]] films internationally from the late 1990s onward. The English-language dubs of [[Princess Mononoke]] and [[Spirited Away]] were the breakthrough — shepherded into US theatres by [[John Lasseter]].
