# Ghibli Museum

Museum in Mitaka, Tokyo, designed largely by [[Hayao Miyazaki]] and opened in 2001. Less an archive than a built-out fantasy: a Cat-Bus, the Robot Soldier from [[Castle in the Sky]] on the roof, a private theatre showing short films you can't see anywhere else.

operated-by:: [[Studio Ghibli]]
designed-by:: [[Hayao Miyazaki]]
