# Toei Animation

Japanese animation studio where [[Hayao Miyazaki]] began his career in 1963 as an in-betweener. He met [[Isao Takahata]] there, and together they participated in the studio's labour-organising. The two would leave Toei in the early 1970s — eventually founding [[Studio Ghibli]] a decade and a half later.
