Privacy

Your atlas lives on your device.

Terra Cognita stores your notes as plain Markdown files in a folder of your choosing. We don't track you, record you, or transmit anything you didn't ask us to. AI features run on-device through Apple's Foundation Models. The only outbound traffic happens when you explicitly request it — fetching a Wikipedia page you pasted, for instance.

  • You own the filesOpen them in any editor. Move them. Back them up. We never see them.
  • No telemetryNo analytics, no crash uploads, no usage stats.
  • No accountsNo login, no cloud sync, no server.
  • AI runs localFoundation Models on-device. Dictation on-device.
