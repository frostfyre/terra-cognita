# Goro Miyazaki

Son of [[Hayao Miyazaki]] and director of [[Tales from Earthsea]] (2006) and *From Up on Poppy Hill* (2011). The first film was made under considerable familial strain — his father reportedly opposed Goro's involvement at the time. The relationship was repaired through the second.
