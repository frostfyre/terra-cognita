# Hiromasa Yonebayashi

Animator and director who joined [[Studio Ghibli]] in 1996. He animated on many of [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s late films before directing *The Secret World of Arrietty* (2010) and *When Marnie Was There* (2014) for Ghibli. Later co-founded Studio Ponoc with other Ghibli alumni.
