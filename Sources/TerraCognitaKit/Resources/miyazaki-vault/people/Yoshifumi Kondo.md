# Yoshifumi Kondo

Animator and director at [[Studio Ghibli]], widely understood as the heir-apparent to [[Hayao Miyazaki]] and [[Isao Takahata]] before his early death in 1998. He directed [[Whisper of the Heart]], his only feature, and served as character designer and animation director on several Ghibli films.
