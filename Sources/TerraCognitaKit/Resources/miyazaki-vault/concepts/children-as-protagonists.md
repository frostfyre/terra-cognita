# Children carrying the story

A Miyazaki conviction: that children can carry moral weight without sentimentalising them. Mei and Satsuki, Chihiro, Sosuke and Ponyo, Mahito in [[The Boy and the Heron]] — the films trust their audiences to take child protagonists seriously.
