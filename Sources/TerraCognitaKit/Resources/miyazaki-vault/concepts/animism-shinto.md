# Animism (Shinto)

Kami — the Shinto sense of spirit-in-everything — runs through most of [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s mature work. Forest spirits in [[Princess Mononoke]], soot sprites and tree gods in [[My Neighbor Totoro]], the entire bathhouse pantheon in [[Spirited Away]]. The films don't argue for Shinto so much as assume its world-view.
