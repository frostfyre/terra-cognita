# Kiki's Delivery Service

Adapted from [[Eiko Kadono]]'s 1985 novel. The Swedish-flavoured European town of Koriko reflects [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s 1971 [[Astrid Lindgren]] research trip — the [[pastoral-european-aesthetic|pastoral imagery]] left over from the abandoned Pippi project. Score by [[Joe Hisaishi]].
