# Whisper of the Heart

Directed by [[Yoshifumi Kondo]] from a [[Hayao Miyazaki]] screenplay — his only feature before his death in 1998. A teenage Tokyo girl chooses her vocation. The film is the most "domestic" entry in the studio's catalogue, and the most clearly about the act of authoring itself.
