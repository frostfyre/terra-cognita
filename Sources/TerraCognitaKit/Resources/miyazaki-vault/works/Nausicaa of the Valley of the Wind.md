# Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind

The film whose box office indirectly funded the founding of [[Studio Ghibli]] the following year. Directed by [[Hayao Miyazaki]], adapted from his own manga serialised in [[Tokuma Shoten]]'s *Animage*. The "God Warrior" sequence was animated by a young [[Hideaki Anno]].

The film fuses every Miyazaki preoccupation: a [[strong-female-protagonists|female protagonist]] flying a glider, a polluted [[environmentalism|toxic forest]], a [[pacifism-anti-war|pacifist heroine]] who refuses the war her elders demand of her, and [[animism-shinto|spirits in the trees]].
