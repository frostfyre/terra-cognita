# Studio Ghibli

Animation studio founded in [[ghibli-founding|1985]] by [[Hayao Miyazaki]], [[Isao Takahata]], and producer [[Toshio Suzuki]] under the wing of publisher [[Tokuma Shoten]]. The name comes from a Saharan wind — a fitting choice for a studio whose work would so often turn on currents of air and flight.

For four decades the studio has held a near-unique position in feature animation: hand-drawn, director-led, internationally distributed but never absorbed. Its films won [[spirited-away-oscar|the Oscar for Best Animated Feature in 2003]] and again in 2024 for [[The Boy and the Heron]].
