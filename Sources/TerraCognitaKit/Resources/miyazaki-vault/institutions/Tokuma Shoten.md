# Tokuma Shoten

Tokyo publisher whose anime magazine *Animage* serialised [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s [[Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind]] manga from 1982. The publisher backed the film's production in 1984 and provided the corporate cover under which [[Studio Ghibli]] was founded the following year. The editor of *Animage* in this period was [[Toshio Suzuki]].
