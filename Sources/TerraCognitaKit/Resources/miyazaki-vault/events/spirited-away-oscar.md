# Spirited Away wins the Oscar

In March 2003 [[Spirited Away]] won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature — the first non-English-language film to do so, and [[Hayao Miyazaki]]'s first Oscar. [[Studio Ghibli]] would win again in 2024 for [[The Boy and the Heron]].
